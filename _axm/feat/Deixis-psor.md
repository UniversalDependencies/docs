Middle Armenian uses `Deixis[psor]` as layered feature for [NOUN]() to encode the position of an entity relative to either the speaker or the hearer.

### <a name="Prox">`Prox`</a>: proximal

The entity is close to the reference point (e.g., to the speaker).

#### Examples

* _Տեսեք, թէ քանի մարդիք <b>յաշխարհէս</b> դարտակ մնացին։/Tesek’, t’ē k’ani mardik’ yašxarhēs dartak mnac’in_ “See how many people were left empty-handed in this world.”,

### <a name="Med">`Med`</a>: medial

The entity is neither close nor far away from the reference point (e.g., from the speaker).

#### Examples

* _Ես այդ <b>աթոռոյդ</b> տէրն  եմ/Es ayd at’oṙoyd tērn em_ “I am the owner of that chair”
