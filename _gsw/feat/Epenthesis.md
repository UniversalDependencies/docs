### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: presence of an epenthetic consonant

The feature is used to indicate words to which an epenthetic consonant was added, for example: _So<b>-n-</b>e scheens Hardfir_ "such a beautiful fire". Epenthetic consonants are added between two vowels at a word boundary to avoid hiatus (in Alsatian, usually _n_, but also _w_).

#### Examples

* _Morn geh<b>w</b>i uf Strosburi._ "Tomorrow I go to Strasbourg."
* _wie er ingfuehrt wùrre<b>-n-</b>isch_ "which was introduced"
