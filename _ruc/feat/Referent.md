Ruuli has a so-called augment, which is a word-initial vowel prefix that commonly occurs on nominals such as nouns, adjectives, some pronouns, etc.
In addition, the augment occurs verb-initially to mark subject relative clauses and can also precede some adverbs.
The use of the augment has been related to referential functions.

<!-- [for augments, including "SBJ.REL"] (ChiBemba: Giv´on 1972; Zulu: von Staden 1973) -->

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: referential

We use `Referent=Yes` to annotate the presence of the augment.

#### Examples

* _<b>o</b>muyembe_ "mango"
