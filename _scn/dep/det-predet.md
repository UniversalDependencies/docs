title: 'det:predet'
shortdef: 'predeterminer'
udver: '2'
---

A predeterminer is the relation between the head of an NP and a word that precedes and modifies the meaning of the NP determiner. In Sicilian this relation is used for the lemma *tuttu*, when it appears in front of another determiner.

~~~ sdparse
Tutti l'amici 
det:predet(amici, Tutti)
~~~
