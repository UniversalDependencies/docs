Ruuli marks focus morphologically.

### <a name="Foc">`Foc`</a>: focus emphasis

Focus is marked with three clitics, _na_, _mbe_, and _te_, and the copula _ni_.
The clitic _na_ marks additive focus (translated as "also" or "even"), 
_te_ is commonly used for sentence focus, 
and _mbe_ is commonly used for predicate focus.
The copula _ni_ is used for argument and adjunct focus. 

#### Examples

* _wee<b>na</b>_ "**even** you (**also** you)"
* _naye<b>te</b>_ "but (focused)"
* _balikwewunyanga<b>mbe</b>_ "they will **just** be surprised"
* _<b>ni</b>kyo nafumbiriirwe_ "that is why I married"
