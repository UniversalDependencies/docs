---
layout: relation
title: 'advmod:lmod'
shortdef: 'locative adverbial modifier'
udver: '2'
---

A locative adverbial modifier is a subtype of the `advmod` relation: if the modifier is specifying a location, it is labeled as `lmod`. E.g.

~~~ sdparse
ესენი არიან რომელნიცა ზედა დათესულნი. 'These are those who were sown upon the rocky place.' მრკ. 4,16 C 
advmod:lmod(დათესულნი-4, ზედა-3)
~~~
