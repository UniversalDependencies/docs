In Cuicatec, this feature applies to [verbs](cux-pos/VERB) and
[auxiliaries](cux-pos/AUX) (value `Fin`), and to property words tagged as
[adjectives](cux-pos/ADJ) (value `Stat`).

### <a name="Fin">`Fin`</a>: finite verb

A finite verb form. Finite verbs are inflected for person, and may carry
aspect prefixes (for example the progressive _ji-_).

#### Examples

* [cux] _<b>Jiyadu</b> jobe._ "De día yo duermo."
* [cux] _Gua <b>deanu</b>._ "No sé."
* [cux] _<b>Nichiy</b> insë._ "Los vio ayer en el mercado."

### <a name="Stat">`Stat`</a>: stative

Stative forms express a property or state rather than an event. They are used
for property concepts such as _cheatea_ "grande" and _lï_ "pequeño", typically
as the predicate of a clause, with no copula.

#### Examples

* [cux] _Bakun <b>cheatea</b>._ "Mi casa es grande."
* [cux] _Baʼa kun gua <b>cheatea</b>._ "Esta casa no es grande."
