Hanging topics are fairly frequent in the Middle Persian corpus. 

- "The son, until (he) gets out of age, the daughter, until (she) takes a husband, the wife, as long as (she is) alive; that one of first-level status has to have subsistence."
~~~ sdparse
pus tā purnāy bawēd duxt tā šōy kunēd zan tā zīndag ān ī pādixšāyīhā xwarišn dārišn \n son until of_full_age becomes daughter until husband makes wife until alive that EZ first_level subsistence hold.NEC
dislocated:topic(dārišn, pus)
mark(bawēd, tā-2)
acl(pus, bawēd)
conj(pus, duxt)
mark(kunēd, tā-6)
acl(duxt, kunēd)
conj(pus, zan)
mark(zīndag, tā-10)
acl(zan, zīndag)
nsubj(dārišn, ān)
obj(dārišn, xwarišn)
~~~

This relation is also used if their is a clausal indirect object, which cannot be annotated in UD.

- "Who wishes to be with (a good) character, tell (him): (You) shall be compassionate!"
~~~ sdparse
kē pad xēm kāmēd būdan gōw kū dildard bawāy \ who with character wishes be tell that compassionate be.2SG.SBJV
nsubj(kāmēd, kē)
dislocated:topic(gōw, kāmēd)
~~~
