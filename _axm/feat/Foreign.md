Boolean feature. Is this a foreign word? Not a loanword but a genuinely foreign word appearing inside native text, e.g. in direct speech, book titles, etc.

This feature would apply either to the [X]() part of speech (unanalyzable token), or to other parts of speech if we know and are willing to annotate the class to which the word belongs in its original language.


### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is foreign

#### Examples

* _Խոտ մի կայ, որ հոռոմերէն <b>ասլօսխօնօս</b> ասեն, որ թարգմանի շատահնդիկ_/_Xot mi kay, or hoṙomerēn aslòsxònòs asen, or t’argmani šatahndik_ “There is a plant called <b>asloskhonos</b> in Greek, which means “many-seeded””,
* _Փարսին <b>շահ ռահ</b> ասէ, և արապն <b>արբի սուլթան</b> և ըստ մերս արքունի ճանապարհ_/_Pʼarsin <b>šah ṙah</b> asē, ew arapn <b>arbi sowltʼan</b> ew ëst mers arkʼowni č̣anaparh_ “In Persian it is called <b>shah rah</b>, in Arabic <b>arbi sultan</b>, and in our language, “royal road””.
