The potential auxiliary of a clause is a form of the verbs **کېدل _kedë́l_ “to become”**
or **کول _kawë́l_ “to do”** used to construct the [potential mood](ps-feat/Mood).

The auxiliary **کېدل _kedë́l_ “to become”** depends with the `aux:pot` relation on the main verb in the conditional form.

In longer potential forms, the auxiliary **کېدل _kedë́l_ “to become”** in the conditional form
and the auxiliary **کول _kawë́l_ “to do”** depend with the `aux:pot` relation on the main verb in the subjunctive form.
