### <a name="Clf">`Clf`</a>: classifier

Chinese classifiers between cardinal numbers and nouns, or between determiners and nouns.

#### Examples

* 三<b>項</b>工程 / _sān <b>xiàng</b> gōngchéng_ “three projects”
