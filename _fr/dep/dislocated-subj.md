title: 'dislocated:subj'
shortdef: 'dislocated subject'

---

The `dislocated:subj` relation is used between a dislocated subject and its head. 


~~~ sdparse
l' avion c' était long . \n The plane it was long.
dislocated:subj(long, avion)
~~~
