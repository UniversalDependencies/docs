The dependent of an `xcomp:desid` relation is an open clausal desiderative compliment, which is headed by a desiderative infinitive ([VerbForm]()=`Inf`).

~~~ sdparse
Waiʔmameiʔpa sinuʔnɨŋ . \n Wear.INF.DESID he_doesn't_want .
xcomp:desid(sinuʔnɨŋ, Waiʔmameiʔpa)
xcomp:desid(he_doesn't_want, Wear.INF.DESID)
~~~
