The `Subcat` feature applies to [VERB](_oge/pos/VERB). Georgian verb can be intransitive, indirect transitive and transitive.

### <a name="Intr">`Intr`</a>: intransitive verb

A verb that does not take arguments other than the subject.

#### Examples

* _ვზი_ 'I sit', _სდგა_ 'he/she stands' etc.

### <a name="Indir">`Indir`</a>: indirect transitive verb

A verb that does not require a direct object but it requires an oblique argument.

#### Examples

* _მოუჴდეს_ 'it happened to him/her', _ესმა_ 'he/she heard (it); it was heard by him/her' etc.

### <a name="Tran">`Tran`</a>: transitive verb

A verb that takes a direct object as argument (in addition to the subject). 

#### Examples

* _ვიხილე_ 'I saw (him/her/it)', _წარგიყვანა_ 'he/she took (someone) away', _შევჰვედრეთ_ 'we implored (him/her/them)' etc.
