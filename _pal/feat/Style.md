This feature takes the following values: Arch, Coll, Expr, Form, Rare, Slng, Vrnc, Vulg

Stylistic features are facultatively added if the context makes it possible to identify them.

### <a name='Arch'>`Arch`: Archaic/obsolete
Some words show variants, but it is not certain whether they are archaic.

### <a name='Coll'>`Coll`: Colloquial
It appears that scribes change the style of language when they write dialogues, but this has not been ascertained yet.

### <a name='Expr'>`Expr`: Expressive/emotional
Speaking that is considered absurd is referred to as "chattering".
- _ud ast=iz ī ka dō māh ast=iš gōwēd padiš <b>drāytar</b> būd_ "And be it even that — if (someone) says "there are two moons" — there was **more chattering** thereon."

### <a name='Form'>`Form`: Formal/literary
In order to formulate a polite request, the verb _framūdan_ "to order" is used in the sense of "to deign", which can simply be translated by "please".
- _ō nēkfrazāmīh paywandīhād ān ī=tān <b>framūd</b> nibištan_ "that what you **graciously** wrote shall be connected to a good end"
- _čē=m andar ašmāh kām kunišn man āgāhēnīd <b>framāyēd</b> pad yazdān kām_ "**Please** inform (me) by the will of the gods what I am supposed to do according to your wish!"

### <a name='Rare'>`Rare`: Rare variant of a word
The feature rare could be used for rare variant of a word.

### <a name='Slng'>`Slng`: Slang
It appears that scribes change the style of language when they write dialogues, but this has not been ascertained yet.

### <a name='Vrnc'>`Vrnc`: Vernacular/dialectal form of a word
Some words show variants, but it is not certain whether they are dialectal.

### <a name='Vulg'>`Vulg`: Vulgar
The word _ǰeh_ "whore" can be used as a pejorative word for a woman.
