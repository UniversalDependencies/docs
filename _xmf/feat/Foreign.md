Boolean feature. Is this a foreign word? Not a loan word and not a foreign name but a genuinely foreign word appearing inside native text, e.g. inside direct speech, titles of books etc. This feature would apply either to the [X](_xmf/pos/X) part of speech (unanalyzable token), or to other parts of speech if we know and are willing to annotate the class to which the word belongs in its original language.


### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is foreign

#### Example 

* _დო მაოთხა <b>საუკუნეს</b> აქ რდუ ფაზისის <b>ისტორიკული აკადემია</b> (dɔ mɑɔtxɑ <b>sɑukʼunɛs</b> ɑk rdu pɑzisis <b>istʼɔrikʼuli ɑkʼɑdɛmiɑ</b>)_ 'In the 4th century, there was the historical academy of Phasis'.
