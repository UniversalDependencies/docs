title: 'obj:appl'
shortdef: 'applied object in applicative construction'
udver: '2'
---

In Ruuli, verbal valency can be increased by the applicative suffix *-ir/-er*. This introduces an additional object argument, typically corresponding to a semantic role that would otherwise be expressed as an oblique dependent.

When the base verb is intransitive, the applied argument is analyzed as the direct object and annotated with the `obj:appl` relation. 

~~~ sdparse
yakoleiree omukama waamwe . \n He_worked_for boss his .
obj:appl(yakoleiree, omukama)
obj:appl(He_worked_for, boss)
~~~
