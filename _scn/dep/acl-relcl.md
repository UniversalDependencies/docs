A relative clause is an instance of <code>acl</code>, characterized by finiteness and usually omission of the modified noun in the embedded clause. The noun can be omitted or, more frequently, substituted by a relative pronoun, relative conjunction, or an adverb. 

~~~ sdparse
Chisti èranu cosi ca iḍḍu avìa a risòrbiri
acl:relcl(cosi, risòrbiri)
~~~
