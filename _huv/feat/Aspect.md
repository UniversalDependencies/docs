In Huave, this feature applies to [verbs](huv-pos/VERB) and to the aspectual proclitic _la_ ([AUX](huv-pos/AUX)).

### <a name="Prog">`Prog`</a>: progressive

The event is in progress. The progressive is marked by a verbal prefix with the forms _te-_ (before the third-person
prefix _a-_: _te-a-tsoj_ "is playing"), _tea-_ (before first-person _na-_: _tea-na-xejmb-each_ "I am bathing him") and
_t-_ (before second-person _er-_: _t-er-ndyak_ "you are speaking"). Herrera Castro (2010) calls it the progressive;
Herrera Castro (2016) calls the same prefix incompletive.

#### Examples

* [huv] _Nop nendok <b>teapaj</b> kos apün nej künch._ "Un pescador está gritando porque lo prensó un cangrejo."
* [huv] _Aaga najtaj kiaj <b>teachench</b> küet._ "Esa señora está asando pescado."

### <a name="Punt">`Punt`</a>: punctual

The event has already happened or the state has already been reached, translated with Spanish "ya". On verbs it is
marked by the prefix _la-_ / _l-_ (Herrera Castro 2010: _l-a-jlüy najngow_ "there is already broth", _l-er-ndyak_
"you already spoke"). When _la_ is attached to a word that is not a verb, the two are split as a multiword token
(_landoj_ = _la_ + _ndoj_) and _la_ carries the feature.

#### Examples

* [huv] _Tikorrül <b>lajntsop</b> noik owil nit._ "En el corral ya brotó una palma."
* [huv] _Apak omeaats xemüm kos <b>laomb</b> mikit nej._ "Mi mamá está contenta porque su gallina ya pone."
* [huv] _Wüx <b>la</b>ndoj aomb xekit, akakaküy._ "Cuando mi gallina ya puso, cacarea."
