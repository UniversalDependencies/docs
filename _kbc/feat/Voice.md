In Kadiwéu, `Voice` is a feature of [verbs](kbc-pos/VERB).

There are three morphological voice markers in Kadiwéu, one for the inverse voice, the other for applicative and antipassive constructions. 

### <a name="Act">`Act`</a>: active

This is the default value.

According to Galves et al.
