Whenever possible, the oblique relation is specified as temporal modifier.

- "For, in the end, your support will be in (your) own deeds." 
~~~ sdparse
čē =t pad frazām abestām ō kunišn ī xwēš bawēd \n because you.OBL in end support to action EZ own becomes
cc(kunišn, čē)
obl(kunišn, =t)
case(frazām, pad)
obl:tmod(kunišn, frazām)
nsubj(kunišn, abestām)
case(kunišn, ō)
det(xwēš, ī)
nmod(kunišn, xwēš)
cop(kunišn, bawēd)
~~~

- "On the fourth day, the demon Wīzareš comes."
~~~ sdparse
rōz ī čahārom wīzareš dēw āyēd \n day EZ fourth Wīzareš demon comes
obl:tmod(āyēd, rōz)
nsubj(āyēd, wīzareš)
flat(wīzareš, dēw)
det(čahārom, ī)
amod(rōz, čahārom)
~~~
