Boolean feature of pronouns. It tells whether the word is possessive.

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is possessive

#### Examples

- possessive pronouns: _ჩემი_ 'my', _შენი_ 'your', _თჳსი_ 'own' etc.
