Reduplication in Sindhi serves a variety of purposes, including
putting emphasis on the reduplicated word. The head is always the first of the reduplicated words.

~~~ sdparse
رستي تي مٿان مٿان ھلڻو آھي. \n road on top top walk is
advmod(مٿان-3, ھلڻو-5)
advmod(top-3, walk-5)
compound:redup(مٿان-3,مٿان-4)
compound:redup(top-3, top-4)
~~~
