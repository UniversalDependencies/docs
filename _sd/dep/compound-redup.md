Reduplication in Sindhi serves a variety of purposes, including
putting emphasis on the reduplicated word or indicating a word and its
antonym. The head is always the first of the reduplicated words.

~~~ sdparse
رستي تي مٿان مٿان ھلڻو آھي. \n road on up up walk is
advmod(مٿان-3, ھلڻو-5)
advmod(up-3, walk-5)
compound:redup( مٿان - 3,   مٿان - 4)
compound:redup(up - 3, up - 4)
~~~
