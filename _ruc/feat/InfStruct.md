Ruuli marks focus morphologically.

### <a name="Foc">`Foc`</a>: focus emphasis

Focus is marked with three clitics, _na_, _mbe_, and _te_, and the copula _ni_.
The clitic _na_ marks additive focus (translated as "also" or "even"), _te_ is common in the expressions of contrast, 
and _mbe_ is common in utterances that comment on a given situation.

The copula _ni_ is used to focus a specific constituent. 

#### Examples

* _wee<b>na</b>_ "<b>even</b> you (<b>also</b> you)"
* _naye<b>te</b>_ "but (focused)"
* _balikwewunyanga<b>mbe</b>_ "they will <b>just</b> be surprised"
* _<b>ni</b>kyo nafumbiriirwe_ "that is why I married"
