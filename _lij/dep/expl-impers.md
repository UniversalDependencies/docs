title: 'expl:impers'
shortdef: 'impersonal expletive'

The `expl:impers` relation is used for impersonal _se_ in clauses with no syntactic subject. The verb shows default third-person singular agreement. In _se perde conoscensa_ “one loses consciousness”, _conoscensa_ remains [obj]().

~~~ conllu
1	se	se	PRON	_	_	2	expl:impers	_	Gloss=one
2	perde	perde	VERB	_	Number=Sing|Person=3	0	root	_	Gloss=loses
3	conoscensa	conoscensa	NOUN	_	_	2	obj	_	Gloss=consciousness

~~~

In reflexive passives with a syntactic subject, the clitic takes [expl:pass]() instead.
