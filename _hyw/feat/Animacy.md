In Western Armenian, animacy is a lexical feature of [nouns](NOUN) and some [pronouns](PRON). We distinguish two values: `Hum` and `Nhum`.

See also the related feature of [Definite]().

### <a name="Hum">`Hum`</a>: human

A subset of animate nouns that includes only human beings (fictional characters, names of professions, and personified characters), but not animals (there may be exceptions that do not fit the class semantically, but belong to it grammatically).

#### Examples

* _մարդ_/_mard_ “man”, _երեխայ_/_erexay_ “child”, _տղայ_/_tġay_ “boy”, _էրիկ_/_ērik_ “husband”, _ուսուցիչ_/_owsowc’ič_ “teacher”, _թագաւոր_/_t’agawor_ “king”, _պայլ_/_payl_ “prince”


### <a name="Nhum">`Nhum`</a>: non-human

This value is used for all non-human referents, including both animals and inanimate entities.

#### Examples

* _տարի_/_tari_ “year”, _յօդուած_/_yòdowaç_ “article”, _լեռ_/_leṙ_ “mountain”, _ծառ_/_çaṙ_ “tree”
* _ձի_/_ji_ “horse”, _կով_/_kov_ “cow”, _առիւծ_/_aṙiwç_ “lion”, _ձուկ_/_jowk_ “fish”
