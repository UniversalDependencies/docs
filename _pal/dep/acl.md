The syntactic relation `attributive clause` is used for non-finite dependent clauses or non-relative clauses of a nominal.

- "the fear of doing (it) sometimes on the ground"
~~~ sdparse
pad zamīg āwāmīhā kirdan bīm \n on ground sometimes doing fear
case(zamīg, pad)
obl:lmod(kirdan, zamīg)
acl(bīm, kirdan)
~~~

- "like sheep, when (they) begin to be afraid because of the wolf's scent"
~~~ sdparse
čiyōn mēš ka az gurg bōy frāz tarsand \n like sheep when from wolf scent forth fear.3pl
mark(tarsand, ka)
acl(mēš, tarsand)
~~~
