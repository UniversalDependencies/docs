A copula is the relation between the nominal predicate _(ստորոգելի)_ and the copular verb _<b>եմ</b>,_ “to be”. The verbs _դառնալ, թվալ, երևալ, ձևանալ, մնալ, հաշվել_ etc. “to become”, despite being counted among copular verbs by some authors (_վերադրառու բայեր, ստորոգելիական վերադիր_),
are not analyzed as `cop`.

We normally take a copula as a dependent of its complement (the nominal predicate).
The nominal predicate is usually a [noun](NOUN), a [pronoun](PRON), an [adjective](ADJ), an [adverb](ADV), or an <a href="../feat/VerbForm.html#inf-infinitive">infinitive</a>.
