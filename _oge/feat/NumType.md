The `NumType` feature applies to [numerals](_oge/pos/NUM).

### <a name="Card">`Card`</a>: cardinal number or corresponding word

#### Examples

* _ერთი_ 'one', _ექუსი_ 'six', Ⴀ 'one', Ⴌ 'fifty' etc.

### <a name="Ord">`Ord`</a>: ordinal number or corresponding word

#### Examples

* _პირველი_ 'the first', _მეორე_ 'the second' etc.

### <a name="Frac">`Frac`</a>: fractional number or corresponding word

#### Examples

_მესამედი_ 'a third part', _მეექუსედე_ 'a six part' etc.

### <a name="Mult">`Mult`</a>: multiple (repetitive) number or corresponding word

#### Examples

* _ორჯერ_ 'twice', _სამჯერ_ 'three times' etc.
