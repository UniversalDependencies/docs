title: 'nsubj:agent'
shortdef: 'agent subject of an actor-voice verb'
udver: '2'
---

A nominal argument of a clause that is in the pivot role and is semantically the 
agent of the clause. This occurs in an actor voice clause.
Though this would be considered unmarked and thus redundant in Indo-European languages, 
in Gorontalo and many other Austronesian languages agent voice is not clearly less 
marked than any other voice, so the label is required.

~~~ sdparse
Ti Dewi lo-tubu uponula \n PIV Dewi cooked the fish
nsubj:agent(lo-tubu, Dewi)
nsubj:agent(cooked, Dewi)
~~~

“Dewi cooked the fish.” (actor voice)
