Reduplication is a common derivation strategy in Ruuli.
It occurs in adverb-to-adverb, verb-to-verb, and adjective-to-adjective derivation.

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: reduplication

In Ruuli, the whole root is reduplicated, which results either in the attenuation or augmentation of the meaning.

#### Examples

* _myukimyuki_ "reddish" (from _myuki_ "red")
