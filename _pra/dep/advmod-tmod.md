title: 'advmod:tmod'
shortdef: 'temporal adverbial modifier'
udver: '2'
---

The _advmod:tmod_ relation is used for temporal adverbal modifiers.

~~~ sdparse
se aja yadā ayaṃ dhaṃma-lipī likhitā tī eva prāṇā ārabhare \n but today when this morality written three only animals killed
advmod:tmod(ārabhare, aja)
advmod:tmod(killed, today)

~~~
