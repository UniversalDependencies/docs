### <a name="Prox">`Prox`</a>: Proximate
Used for near deixis, e.g. _ēn_ "this" or _ēdar_ "here".

### <a name="Remt">`Remt`</a>: Remote / distal
Used for far deixis, e.g. _ān_ "that" or _ānōh_ "there".
