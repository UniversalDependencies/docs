title: 'advmod:lmod'
shortdef: 'advmod with locative modality'
udver: '2'
---

The  _advmod:lmod_ relation is used for adverbial modifiers with locative modality.

~~~ sdparse
Udu tõuseb ülespoole .
advmod:lmod(tõuseb-2, ülespoole-3)
~~~
