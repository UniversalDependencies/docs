Person of the applicative suffix of a verb. The applicative suffixes, with the element -l- (-ለ-, -ሉ, -ላ, …), cross-reference an applied argument such as a beneficiary, maleficiary, instrument, source or location, and form a series distinct from the ordinary object suffixes ([Person[obj]](Person-obj)). The feature records the person of the applicative suffix, independently of the person of the subject (`Person`).

### <a name="1">`1`</a>: first person applied argument

#### Examples

* ዘይሓልፈለይ / *zəyħalfələy* "(the trouble) never passes for me": `Person=3|Person[app]=1` (ti_ref_179)

### <a name="2">`2`</a>: second person applied argument

#### Examples

* ገዚአልካ / *gəziʔɛlka* "I bought (it) for you (masc.)"

### <a name="3">`3`</a>: third person applied argument

#### Examples

* ገዚአሉ / *gəziʔɛlu* "I bought (it) for him": `Person=1|Person[app]=3` (ti_ref_110)
