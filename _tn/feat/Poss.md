In Tswana, `Poss` is a Boolean feature of words tagged as [pronouns](). It tells whether the word is possessive.

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is possesive

#### Examples

The following Tswana words tagged as pronouns have this feature:

* _bone, gago, me_

Note that there is no `No` value. If the word is not possessive, the `Poss` feature will just not be mentioned in the `FEAT` column.
