title: 'dislocated'
shortdef: 'dislocated elements'
udver: '2'
---

The `dislocated` relation is used for fronted or postposed elements that do not fulfill
the usual core grammatical relations of a sentence. These elements often appear to be in the periphery
of the sentence, and may be separated off with a comma intonation. The `dislocated`
element attaches to the head of the clause to which it belongs:

~~~ sdparse
Mano draugas , jis labai protingas . \n My friend , he-is very smart .
dislocated(protingas, draugas)
dislocated(smart, friend)
~~~
