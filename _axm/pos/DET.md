### Definition

Determiners are words that modify [nouns](NOUN) or noun phrases and express the reference of the noun phrase in context. That is, a determiner may indicate whether the noun is referring to a definite or indefinite element of a class, to a closer or more distant element, to an element belonging to a specified person or thing, to a particular number or quantity, etc.

Under this definition, determiners include both _articles_ and _pro-adjectives (pronominal adjectives)_, such as _այս/ays, այդ/ayd, ամենայն/amenayn, մէկայլ/mēkayl, միւս/miws_. An important point to note is that most determiners are traditionally called pronouns; that is, a UD-conformant annotation of Middle Armenian must distinguish between substantive pronouns (UD tag [PRON]()) and attributive pronouns (UD tag `DET`).

Also note that the `DET` tag includes (pronominal) _quantifiers_ (words like _հանչաք, հէնչափ/hančak’, hēnčap’, ամէն/amēn&nbsp;_ “that many, every”).

Note that in Middle Armenian a nominal usually allows one `DET` modifier, but there are occasional cases of _addeterminers_, which appear outside the usual determiner, such as _այն_ in _<b>այն</b> ամէն ջիլքն մարմնոյն/ayn amēn ǰilk’n marmnoyn&nbsp;_ “all <b>those</b> tendons in the body.” In such cases, both _այն/ayn_ “this” and _ամէն/amēn_ “all” are given the POS `DET`.

### Examples

- indefinite article: _մի/mi_ “a, an”
- possessive determiners: _իմ/im, քո(քոյ, քու)/k’o(k’oy, kow), իր(իւր)/ir(iwr), մեր/mer, ձեր/jer, իրենց/irenc’ (իրանց/iranc’, իւրեանց/iwreanc’, իւրենց/iwrenc’)_ “my, your, his/her/its, our, your, their”
- demonstrative determiners: _այս/ays_ “this”, as in _Թո՛ղ զսէր աշխարհիս <b>այս</b> անցւորական:/T’o՛ġ zsēr ašxarhis ays anc’worakan_ “Leave love to <b>this</b> fleeting world.”
- interrogative determiners: _ո՞ր/or_ “which?”, as in _<b>ո՞ր</b> տեղւոյ օդն առողջ եւ բարեյարմար է/or teġwoy òdn aṙoġǰ ew bareyarmar ē_ “Where (lit. in <b>which</b> place) is the air healthy and pleasant?”
- relative determiners: _որ/or_ “which”, as in _այն տեղն <b>որ</b> մօտ է ʼի ծովն/ayn teġn or mòt ē ʼi çovn_ “The place <b>which</b> is close to the sea”
- relative possessive determiners: _ում(յում)/owm(yowm)_, _որոյ(որու)/oroy(orow)_ “whose”
- indefinite determiners: _այլ/ayl, մէկ մի/mēk mi, մէկայլ/mēkayl, միւս/miws_ “other/some, another, (the) other”
- total determiners: _ամէն/amēn_, _ամէն մէկ/amēn mēk_ “every”
