---
layout: relation
title:  'advcl:relcl'
shortdef : 'adverbial relative clause modifier'
udver: '2'
---

This relation applies to a relative clause that is adverbial in one of the following ways:

(1) It modifies a clause (as opposed to typical relative clauses, which are adnominal and use [acl:relcl]()).

~~~ sdparse
Ke okilwe ke madi a monna yo o neng a ratana le Dimpho
I was deceived by the money of the man who was in love with Dimpho
advcl:relcl(monna, ratana)
mark(ratana, yo)
~~~

See [details on advcl-relcl](advl-relcl.html).
