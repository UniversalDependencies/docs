Degree of comparison is a grammatical feature of qualitative [adjectives](hy-pos/ADJ) and some [adverbs](hy-pos/ADV).

The comparative (second) degree is formed periphrastically with the adverb _այլ (էլ)_ “more” e.g. _այլ աղէկ_/_ayl aġēk_ “better”; _այլ ծանդր_/_ayl çandr_ “heavier”. 

Other adverbs such as _դհա_/_dha,_ _ջիլիզ_/_jiliz_ may be used as intensifiers of an attribute.

### <a name="Pos">`Pos`</a>: positive, first degree

This is the base form that merely states a quality of something, without comparing it to qualities of others. Note that although this degree is traditionally called "positive", negative properties can be compared, too.

#### Examples

* _<b>հին</b> սիգեղ_/_hin sigeġ_ “<b>old</b> seal”
* _<b>պակաս</b> զգայութիւն_/_pakas zgayowt’iwn_ “<b>little</b> sensitivity”
* _ծարավն <b>շատ</b> լինի_/_çaravn šat lini_ Lit.: “thirst is <b>much</b>”

### <a name="Cmp">`Cmp`</a>: comparative, second degree

The quality of one object is compared to the same quality of another object.
The adverb _այլ_/_ayl_ and the intensifiers _դհա_/_dha_ and _ջիլիզ_/_jiliz_ are used to form periphrastic comparative degree of adjectives and adverbs. Note that only these three adverbs have the `Cmp` feature.

#### Examples

* _<b>այլ</b> հին_/_ayl hin_ “<b>older</b>”
* _Իր սիգեղն <b>այլ</b> հին է քան զայլ անսիզվորացդ_/_Ir sigeġn ayl hin ē k’an zayl ansizvorac’d_ “His seal is <b>older</b> than of those other owners.”
* _<b>այլ</b> պակաս_/_ayl pakas_ “<b>less</b>”
* _այն <b>այլ</b> պակաս է_/_ayn ayl pakas ē_ “That is <b>less, fewer</b>”
* _<b>այլ</b> շատ_/_ayl šat_ “<b>more</b>”
* _ծարավն <b>այլ</b> շատ լինի_/_çaravn ayl šat lini_ Lit.: “thirst is <b>yet more</b>”

### <a name="Sup">`Sup`</a>: superlative, third degree

The quality of one object is compared to the same quality of all other objects within a set. 
It is formed periphrastically with the adverb _այլ_/_ayl_ “more” and the positive form of the adjective with the definite article _ն_/_n_ (enclitic). In later Middle Armenian texts it may also be expressed by a periphrastic superlative construction using the inflected pronoun _ամենից_/_amenic’_ “most of all”. 

#### Examples

* _<b>այլ ջրախառն</b> գինին_/_ayl ǰraxaṙn ginin_ “the most diluted wine (with water)”
* _<b>այլ</b> աղէկ<b>ն</b>_/_ayl aġēkn_ “the best”
* _<b>ամենից գիտուն</b>_/_amenic’ gitown_ “the <b>cleverest</b>”.

Note that there is no special morphological form for the absolute superlative in Middle Armenian. To express this meaning, adverbs such as _կարի_/_kari_ “extremely”, _խիստ_/_xist_ “highly”, _պինդ_/_pind_ “strongly”, _շատ_/_šat_ “very” are used.
