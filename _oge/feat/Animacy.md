Animacy is a lexical feature of nouns. In Old Georgian, it is not marked morphologically and does not influence number agreement. Therefore, it is best understood as a semantic, rather than a morphosyntactic, category.

The `Animacy` feature applies to [NOUN](_oge/pos/NOUN) and [PROPN](_oge/pos/PROPN).

### <a name="Anim">`Anim`</a>: animate nouns.

Human beings, animals, fictional characters, names of professions etc. are normally animate. 

#### Examples

* _იაკობ_ 'Jacob' etc.

### <a name="Inan">`Inan`</a>: inanimate nouns.

Nouns that are not animate are inanimate.

#### Examples

* _ქალაქ, ქალაქი_ 'city' etc.
