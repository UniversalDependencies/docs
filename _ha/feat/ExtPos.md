This feature indicates, for the head of a [fixed expression](https://universaldependencies.org/en/dep/fixed.html) or idioms,
the effective UPOS of the full expression when it differs from the UPOS of the word itself.
Occasionally, `ExtPos` is used even if `ExtPos` = `upos`.

It is also used to account for polyfunctionality, such as basic ADP functioning as ADV, SCONJ or CCONJ.

`ExtPos` is used in the [SUD](https://surfacesyntacticud.org/) framework.
It is kept in FEATS in the UD conversion.

### <a name="ADP">`ADP`</a>: adposition-like expression

#### Examples

* _zaːkà gan tà <b>à geːfèn</b> tiːtìː maː //_ "You will see it by the roadside."

### <a name="ADV">`ADV`</a>: adverb-like expression

#### Examples

* _<b>sai dà</b> akà ƙaːràƙè aikìn nan dà dàddareː //_ "sai dà akà ƙaːràƙè aikìn nan dà dàddareː //"

### <a name="CCONJ">`CCONJ`</a>: coordinating conjunction-like expression

#### Examples

* _àkwai su Àdàːmu, <b>sai</b> Mustàfa //_ "there is Adamu, <b>and then</b> Mustafa"

### <a name="NOUN">`NOUN`</a>: noun-like expression

#### Examples

* _yaƙ ƙi <b>hwaɗìː</b>_ "He refused to answer"

### <a name="PRON">`PRON`</a>: pronoun-like expression

#### Examples

* _yànzu munàː dà gàrmaː <b>wandà yakèː</b> munàː haɗàːwaː dà shaːnuː_ "Now, we have a plough that we use with cows."

### <a name="SCONJ">`SCONJ`</a>: subordinator-like expression

#### Examples

* _mazàunin gàrin Pallàːdân <b>kàman yandà</b> na taɓà faɗìn makà à farkoː_ "I live in Palladan as I told you at first."

### <a name="VERB">`VERB`</a>: verb-like expression

#### Examples

* _tòː yà <b>ci gàba</b> mànà !//_ "Well, let him continue!"
