### Auxiliary particle بۀ _bë_
The future auxiliary of a clause is word used to construct the future tense.
For imperfective future, it depends with the `aux:fut` relation on the main verb in the present indicative form.
For perfective future, it depends with the `aux:fut` relation on the main verb in the subjunctive form.
