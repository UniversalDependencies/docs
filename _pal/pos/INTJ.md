### Definition
Interjections are rare in the written corpus of Middle Persian. They are words that are used as an exclamation or part of an exclamation without specific lexical content. They are not syntactically related to other accompanying expressions.

### Examples
- *drāyist gannāgmēnōy ō dēwān kū <b>āy</b> wād murnǰēnēd ī čēr ī tagīg ī ohrmazddād* "The Foul Spirit yelled to the demons: Hey! Destroy the wind, the magnificent, swift (one) created by Ohrmazd!"

### Border cases
Words with lexical meaning that are used in exclamations are not annotated as interjections because this usage does not constitute a separate sense (e.g. *wāy* 'woe').
