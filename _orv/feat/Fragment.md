In historical texts, indicates a word form that is not fully visible (not readable). Usually such words are found in manuscripts that are torn, have abrasions and other losses. 
Lemma and grammatical annotation of such words can be based on the intended reading of the token taking into account visible fragment(s) and context. 

The feature should not be mixed with [Typo]()`=Yes`, which is intended to mark missplelling and mispronunciation.  

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is fragmented

#### Examples

* _<b>вз…</b>_ (the beginning of the word взѧти `take')
