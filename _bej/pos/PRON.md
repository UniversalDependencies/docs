### Definition

Pronouns are words that substitute for [nouns](NOUN) or noun phrases.
Their meaning is recoverable from the linguistic or extralinguistic context.
Pronoun can also be clitics, affixes.

### Examples

- _hoː_ 1SG
- _=heːb_ 1SG  
- _-na_ 1PL
