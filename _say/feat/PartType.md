### <a name="Adv">`Adv`</a>: Adverbial particle

#### Examples
 * _əndá kətá mbwáː <b>ɗi</b>_ "Then you arrived.."

### <a name="Case">`Case`</a>: Case particle

#### Examples
 * _tə́ gə̀m tə̀ <b>mə́n</b> ɗi "they gave it to him"

### <a name="Foc">`Foc`</a>: In-situ focus particle

#### Examples
 * _dèːwôs ɓét <b>kəndí_</b> "That's its only reason."

### <a name="Ill">`Ill`</a>: Illocution particle

#### Examples
 * _màː ɗutíː <b>ŋǎːn</b>_ "Didn't we kill him?"

### <a name="Neg">`Neg`</a>: Negation particle

#### Examples
 * _tôː mə́ yîsə́n <b>hŋ́</b>_ "Well we didn't know."

### <a name="Pred">`Pred`</a>: Predicating particle
The two predicating particles _àkwey_ "there is" and _bâː_ "there isn't" are Hausa loanwords.

#### Examples
 * _<b>àkwéy</b> dwaːmí gón [...]_ "there is a metal [...]"

### <a name="Top">`Top`</a>: Topic particle

#### Examples
 * _myâːn <b>kúmá</b> máː ye màtsàlêʃɩ́ː_ "As for me, I see the problems."

### <a name="Disc">`Disc`</a>: Discourse particle

#### Examples
 * _<b>tôː</b> ɮǐːwôs ʧol-ʧól_ "Well, his body is very smooth."
