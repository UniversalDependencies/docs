### Definition

Nouns are a part of speech typically denoting a person, place, thing,
animal or idea.

The `NOUN` tag is intended for common nouns only. See [PROPN]() for
proper nouns and [PRON]() for pronouns.

Pashto nouns have the lexical feature [ps-feat/Gender]().
Furthermore, the nouns inflect for [ps-feat/Number]() and [ps-feat/Case]().

A verbal noun can be derived productively from almost every verb
(e.g. لیکل _likë́l_ “to write” → لیکنه _likë́na_ “writing”).
It is tagged `NOUN`, but with `VerbForm=Vnoun` feature.
It has always the feminine gender and the full number-case inflectional paradigm.

Adjectives and participles, already turned into nouns and used in that way, are also tagged `NOUN`.

For the overview of declension of nouns in Pashto, see [Pashto nominal system](nominal-system.html).
