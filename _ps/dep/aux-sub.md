The subjunctive auxiliary of a clause is a subjunctive form of the verb **کېدل _kedë́l_ “to become”**
used to construct an irregular [subjunctive mood](ps-feat/Mood) of the irregular verb **تلل _tlël_ “to go”**.
It depends with the `aux:sub` relation on the special form **لاړ _lâṛ_** or **ولاړ _wlâṛ_** of the main verb.
Other verbs have simple subjunctive forms.
