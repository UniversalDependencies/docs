---

Bahuvrīhi compounds are exocentric compounds indicating a possessive relationship:

~~~ sdparse
sa- devake \n with gods

compound:bv(devake, sa-)
compound:bv(gods, with)
~~~

A negative possessive relationship would also be *bahuvrīhi*:

~~~ sdparse
nip- purisehi \n without men

compound:bv(purisehi, nip-)
compound:bv(men, without)
~~~
