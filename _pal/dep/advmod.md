Adverbial modifiers includes sentential and verbal [adverbs](ADV) as well as [particles](PART). They typically modify verbs but can also depend on nouns, especially action nouns.

- "in the hope of peace in paradise, (they) willingly take on a great deal of trouble"
~~~ sdparse
ēmēd ī ō rāmišn ī pad wahišt rāy was ranǰ kāmagīhā ō grīw padīrēnd \n hope EZ to peace EZ in paradise for much trouble willingly to neck accept.3PL
case(ēmēd, rāy)
det(ranǰ, was)
obj(padīrēnd, ranǰ)
advmod(padīrēnd, kāmagīhā)
case(grīw, ō-12)
obl(padīrēnd, grīw)
~~~

- "(chapter) on the good reputation here (i.e. in this world), the deliverance there (i.e. hereafter), the reward in both of them"
~~~ sdparse
abar husrawīh ēdar bōzišn ī ānōh pādāšn ī andar harw dō \n on good_reputation here deliverance EZ there reward EZ in every two
advmod(husrawīh, ēdar)
det(ānōh, ī)
advmod(bōzišn, ānōh)
det(dō, ī-8)
case(dō, andar)
det(dō, harw)
nmod(pādāšn, dō)
~~~

- "pleading requests"
~~~ sdparse
xwāhišn ī lābakkarīhā \n requesting EZ pleadingly
det(lābakkarīhā, ī)
advmod(xwāhišn, lābakkarīhā)
~~~
