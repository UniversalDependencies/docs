title: 'nmod:arg'
shortdef: 'required nominal dependent of a noun'
udver: '2'
---

Non-agreeing attributes, that are governed by verbal nouns are labeled as nmod:arg.  

~~~ sdparse
Atstovavimas vargšams labai svarbus . \n Representation of-the-poor is very important .
nmod:arg(Atstovavimas, vargšams)
nmod:arg(Representation, of-the-poor)
~~~
