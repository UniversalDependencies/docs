The `cop` relation links the copular verb _ëse_ “to be” to the predicate, which is the head of the clause. The predicate may be nominal, adjectival, prepositional, adverbial or [clausal](nsubj-outer.html).

~~~ conllu
1	t’	_	PRON	_	_	4	nsubj	_	Gloss=you|SpaceAfter=No
2	ê	_	AUX	_	_	4	cop	_	Gloss=are
3	unna	_	DET	_	_	4	det	_	Gloss=a
4	persoña	_	NOUN	_	_	0	root	_	Gloss=person

~~~

~~~ conllu
1	a	_	DET	_	_	2	det	_	Gloss=the
2	scarpa	_	NOUN	_	_	6	nsubj	_	Gloss=shoe
3	a	_	PRON	_	_	6	expl	_	Gloss=it
4	l’	_	PART	_	_	6	dep	_	SpaceAfter=No
5	é	_	AUX	_	_	6	cop	_	Gloss=is
6	streita	_	ADJ	_	_	0	root	_	Gloss=tight

~~~

In existential constructions such as _gh’é_ “there is”, by contrast, _ëse_ is the verbal head of the clause. For locative _lì_ “there” with `cop`, see the [contrastive example under advcl:relcl](advcl-relcl.html#locative-predicate).
