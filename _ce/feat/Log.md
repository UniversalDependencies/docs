The feature `Log` is used to identify logophoric pronouns, i.e. pronouns used in direct speech to refer to the reported speaker. 
In Chechen, logophoric pronouns are identical to regular third-person reflexive pronouns, e.g., the absolutive _shaa_ and the ergative/oblique _shie_. 

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: logophoric

The value is applied to a third-person reflexive pronoun when it functions logophorically in direct speech to refer to the reported speaker.

#### Examples

* _lielosh ju <b>shie<\b>_ "(He) treated <b>me<\b> like that" (`Log`=`Yes` on _shie_, which is identical in form with the third person reflexive pronoun.)
