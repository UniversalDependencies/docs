title: 'advmod:deg'

The _advmod:deg_ relation is used for adverbal modifiers of degree.

~~~ sdparse
Кӧть луныс вӧлі зэв мича. \n Even though the day had been quite beautiful.
advmod:deg(мича, зэв)

~~~
