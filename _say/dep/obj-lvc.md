The `obj:lvc` relation is used for objects complements within light verb constructions.

~~~ sdparse
de dey do research every time \n They are doing research all the time.
obj:lvc(do,research)
~~~

See examples in [UD_Zaar-Autogramm](http://match.grew.fr/?corpus=UD_Zaar-Autogramm@conv&relation=obj:lvc).
