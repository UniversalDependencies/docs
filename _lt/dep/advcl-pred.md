title: 'advcl:pred'
shortdef: 'optional depictive'
udver: '2'
---

Beginning with release 2.17, optional depictives (secondary predication) are annotated with the dedicated relation
subtype `advcl:pred` instead of plain `advcl`.

Adjectives can serve as optional depictives; nevertheless, nominals are also frequent in this
function, in particular with the conjunction _kaip_ “like, as”.

See [u-dep/xcomp]() for further discussion of resultatives and depictives.

~~~ sdparse
Tą dieną prisimenu kaip vieną gražiausių šį mėnesį . \n I remember that day as one of the most beautiful this month .
advcl:pred(prisimenu, vieną)
advcl:pred(remember, one)
~~~
