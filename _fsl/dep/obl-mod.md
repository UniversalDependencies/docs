The `obl:mod` relation is used to encode oblique modifiers.

~~~ sdparse
PNT[MOI] [PROCHE] [DEUX] [MINUTE] [MARCHER] [TRAM] \n Next by, in a 2 minute walk, there is the tram
obl:mod ([MARCHER], [MINUTE])
~~~
