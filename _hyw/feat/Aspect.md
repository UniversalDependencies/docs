Aspect is a feature that specifies the temporal structure of an action, such as its duration or whether it is completed. In Western Armenian, some tenses are combinations of tense and aspect. 

Note that some aspectual meanings in (secondary) compound tenses may be expressed at participles.

### <a name="Imp">`Imp`</a>: imperfect aspect

The action took, takes, or will take place over a period of time, without specifying whether or when it was, is, or will be completed.

#### Examples

* _նոյն համոզումը <b>ունէր</b>_/_noyn hamozowmë <b>ownēr</b>_ “He/She shared the same conviction.”,
* _որոշապէս թուականը չեմ <b>յիշեր</b>_/_orošapēs t’owakanë čem <b>yišer</b>_ “I don't remember the exact date”,
* _Կայ խոշոր տարբերութիւն մը_/_Kay xošor tarberowt’iwn më_ “There is a big difference.”


### <a name="Perf">`Perf`</a>: perfect aspect

The action is presented as completed. Since this aspect focuses on the point of completion, it does not work well with the present tense.

#### Examples

* _պուլկարական բանակը <b>թողուցի ու փախայ</b>_/_powlkarakan banakë <b>t’oġowc’i ow p’axay</b>_ “I left the Bulgarian army and fled”,
* _շատ ուշ <b>ճանչցած</b> էր զայն_/_šat owš <b>č̣ančc’aç</b> ēr zayn_ “he/she had recognized him/her very late”,
* _այլեւս <b>մնացեր</b> էր հոն_/_aylews <b>mnac’er</b> ēr hon_ “he/she had stayed there for good”.


### <a name="Prosp">`Prosp`</a>: prospective aspect

In general, prospective aspect can be described as relative future: the action is/was/will be expected to take place at a moment that follows the reference point; the reference point itself can be in past, present or future.

In some contexts, subjunctive and conditional forms may also indicate prospective aspect and signal an event that is expected to take place. The prospective meaning is especially common in conditional imperfect forms.

The future participle also indicates prospective aspect.

#### Examples

* _ալ անկարելի է որ հոս <b>մնամ</b>_/_al ankareli ē or hos <b>mnam</b>_ “it is no longer possible for me to stay here”,
* _երբոր խօսքս <b>աւարտեմ</b>, պիտի թողուք որ <b>մեկնիմ</b>_/_erbor xòsk’s <b>awartem</b>, piti t’oġowk’ or <b>meknim</b>_ “when I finish my speech, you must let me leave”,
* _մեր <b>գիտնալիք</b> բանը չէ_/_mer <b>gitnalik’</b> banë čē_ “it is none of our business, it is not for us to know”,
* _բան մը ունէր <b>ըսելիք</b>_/_ban më ownēr <b>ëselik’</b>_ “he/she had something to say”,
* _դեռ բաւական բաներ մը ունէին <b>պատրաստելիք</b>_/_deṙ bawakan baner më ownēin <b>patrastelik’</b>_ “they still had quite a few things to prepare”.


### <a name="Prog">`Prog`</a>: progressive aspect

The action is in progress with respect to the current or a reference time. The (AUX_)[] `կոր` indicates progressive aspect.

#### Examples

* _սարսափելի օրերու մէջ կ՚ապրինք <b>կոր</b>_/_sarsap’eli òrerow mēǰ k՚aprink’ <b>kor</b>_ “we are living through horrifying days”,
* _չեմ կրնար <b>կոր</b> կատարել պարտականութիւնս_/_čem krnar <b>kor</b> katarel partakanowt’iwns_ “I cannot fulfill my duty right now”,
* _բայց ան կը սիրէ <b>կոր</b> քեզ_/_bayc’ an kë sirē <b>kor</b> k’ez_ “but he/she keeps loving you”.


### <a name="Dur">`Dur`</a>: durative aspect

The situation extends over a period of time without reference to its endpoint.

The subject participle expresses durative aspect.

#### Examples

* _խաւարի մէջ <b>ապրող</b> դիւային էակ մը_/_xawari mēǰ <b>aproġ</b> diwayin ēak më_ “a demonic creature living in the dark”,
* _ասդին անդին քուրջեր <b>հաւաքող</b> մարդիկ_/_asdin andin k’owrǰer <b>hawak’oġ</b> mardik_ “people gathering rags here and there”,
* _պատուհանէն <b>հոսող</b> առատ լոյսին տակ_/_patowhanēn <b>hosoġ</b> aṙat loysin tak_ “under the abundant light streaming from the window”.


### <a name="Iter">`Iter`</a>: iterative

Denotes repeated action, with multiplicative or distributive meanings. Western Armenian has three main iterative markers _-ատ_/_-at_, _-ըտ_/_-ët_, _-ուըռտ_/_-owëṙt_, _-կլտ_/_-klt_, _-կոտ_/_-kot_, _-կռտ_/_-kṙt_, _-շտկ_/_-štk_, _-ոտ_/_-ot_, _-ուըտ_/_-owët_, _-ռտ_/_-ṙt_, _-տ_/_-t_, _-տկ_/_-tk_ or _root reduplication_.

Note that in Western Armenian iterative is considered as a lexical feature of [verbs](VERB), thus they have morphologically related non-iterative counterparts, but it is not a regular system and the two verbs are represented by different lemmas. We mark them as biaspectual.

#### Examples

* _թռչիլ_/_t’ṙčil_ “to jump”, _<b>թռչկոտիլ</b>_/_<b>t’ṙčkotil</b>_ “to hop”,
* _կտրել_/_ktrel_ “to cut”, _<b>կտրտել</b>_/_<b>ktrtel</b>_ “to chop, to dice”,
* _կռուիլ_/_kṙowil_ “to quarrel”, _<b>կռուըտիլ</b>_/_<b>kṙowëtil</b>_ “to bicker, to squabble”.
