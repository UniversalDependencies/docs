The dependent of an `advcl:cntf` relation is a counterfactual comparison adverbial clause, which is headed by a counterfactual converb ([VerbForm]()=`Cnv`, [ConvType]()=`Cntf`).

2-3	likhilo	_	_	_	_	_	_	_	_
2	likhi	likhi	ADP	EQU	_	1	mark	_	Gloss=EQU|MSeg=likhi
3	lo	lo	PART	SURP	InfStruct=Foc	1	advmod:emph	_	Gloss=SURP|MSeg=lo
4	nɨŋno	nɨŋs	VERB	_	Aspect=Imp|Mood=Ind|Number=Sing|Person=3|Tense=Pres|VerbForm=Fin|Voice=Act	0	root	_	Gloss=seem-IND.NPST[.3SG.S]|MSeg=nɨŋs-nok|SpaceAfter=No
5	.	.	PUNCT	_	_	4	punct	_	SpacesAfter=\n
