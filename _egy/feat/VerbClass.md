There are verbs with 1 to 6 radicals (Schenkel 2012: 183). If all radicals are strong, the abbreviation _lit_ (literal) is used, for example _č̣ṭ_ “say” is a 2-lit. verb. If the last radical is weak, the abbreviation _inf_ (infirma i.e. weak) is used, for example _mri̯_ “love” is a 3ae-inf verb. Verbs with a reduplicated radical are labelled with the abbreviation _red_ (reduplicata) and causative verbs with the abbreviation _caus_.

### <a name="2lit">`2lit`</a>: verb with 2 radicals, both strong

#### Examples

* _<b>č̣ṭ</b>_ “say”

### <a name="3aeinf">`3aeinf`</a>: verb with 3 radicals, the last one weak

#### Examples

* _<b>mri̯</b>_ “love”
