In Kadiwéu, first-person singular pronouns distinguish the speaker's gender. The feature describes the speaker, not the pronoun's referent's gender.

### <a name="Fem">`Fem`</a>: feminine speaker

#### Examples

* [kbc] _eeyo_ ‘I’ `Gender[spkr]=Fem`

### <a name="Masc">`Masc`</a>: masculine speaker

#### Examples

* [kbc] _ee_ ‘I’ `Gender[spkr]=Masc`

Griffiths (2002: 40–41) identifies _eeyo_ as the feminine-speaker form and _ee_ as the masculine-speaker form (entries _eeyo_ and _ee_). This feature is distinct from `Gender`, which marks nominal gender.

## References

* Griffiths, Glyn. 2002. _Dicionário da língua Kadiwéu_. Sociedade Internacional de Linguística, pp. 40–41.
