Reduplication is a common means to express distribution ("one X each"), separation (e.g. "sit separately"), variety, diversity, or emphasis.

- "taste of different kinds"
~~~ sdparse
mizag ī gōnag gōnag \n taste EZ type type
compound:redup(gōnag-3, gōnag-4)
nmod(mizag, gōnag-3)
~~~

- "They, each, are born in the same way."
~~~ sdparse
awēšān ēk ēk pad ham gōnag ēdōn zāyēnd \n they one one in same type thus are_born
compound:redup(ēk-2, ēk-3)
dislocated:res(zāyēnd, ēk-2)
~~~

- "Then (it) seemed more sensible to me to separate and sort the questions topic by topic."
~~~ sdparse
ēg =im pursišn dar dar ǰudāg ud wizihāgīhātar kirdan čimīgtar sahist \n then me question topic topic separate and more_assortedly making more_reasonable seemed
compound:redup(dar-4, dar-5)
obl(kirdan, dar-4)
~~~
