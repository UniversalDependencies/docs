### <a name="Prep">`Prep`</a>: Preposition
An adposition that precedes its complement or the prepositional part of a circumposition.

#### Examples
- _<b>pad</b> ān zamān_ "**at** that time"
- _<b>pad</b> čē kār rāy_ "**for** what task" (lit. at what task for)

### <a name="Post">`Post`</a>: Postposition
An adposition that follows its complement or the second part of a circumposition.

#### Examples
- _dānāgīh ud frazānagīh <b>rāy</b>_ "**because of** (his) knowledge and intelligene"
- _pad čē kār <b>rāy</b>_ "**for** what task" (lit. at what task for)

### <a name="Circ">`Circ`</a>: Circumposition
This is only used together with `Prep` or `Post` to mark a circumposition.

#### Examples:
- _<b>pad</b> čē kār <b>rāy</b>_ "**for** what task" (lit. at what task for)
