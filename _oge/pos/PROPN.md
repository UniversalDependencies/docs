### Definition

A proper noun is a noun that is the name (or part of the name) of a unique entity, be it an individual, a place, or an object.

The `PROPN` tag is intended for proper nouns only. See [NOUN](_oge/pos/NOUN) for common nouns.


### Corresponding language-specific part-of-speech tags

Noun Prop:	Proper noun


### Examples

_იაკობ_ *iakob* 'Jacob'
_მცხეთა_ *mcʻxetʻa* 'Mtskheta'
_ლიახვი_ *liaxvi* 'Liakhvi'
