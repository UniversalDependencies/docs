This subtype of [compound]() covers a range of reduplicated forms in Middle Armenian.
Reduplication is a common process especially for [adverbs](ADV) and [adjectives](ADJ), but it is also used for reduplicated [noun](NOUN) and [verb](VERB) forms. They can have additional feature of [Echo]().

~~~ sdparse
Որ զմեզ աժնեց բաժնեց կամ ի մեր մէջն զրուցեց ։ \n Or zmez ažnec’ bažnec’ kam i mer mēǰn zrowc’ec’ . \n Who separated - divided us, or sowed [discordant] talk between us .
compound:redup(աժնեց, բաժնեց)
compound:redup(separated, divided)
~~~

~~~ sdparse
Բուռն բուռն ձգէ, որ ուտէ ։ \n Bowṙn bowṙn jgē, or owtē . \n Throw it handful by-handful, so that he/she/it may eat.
compound:redup(Բուռն, բուռն)
compound:redup(handful, by-handful)
~~~

~~~ sdparse
Մէջ ընդ մէջ և ժամ ժամ դեղին դառնայ ։ \n Mēǰ ënd mēǰ ew žam žam deġin daṙnay . \n From-time to time and hour by-hour it turns yellow .
compound:redup(Մէջ, մէջ)
compound:redup(From-time, time)
compound:redup(ժամ, ժամ)
compound:redup(hour, by-hour)
~~~
