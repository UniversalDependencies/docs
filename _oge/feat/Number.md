`Number` is an inflectional feature of [nouns](_oge/pos/NOUN), [proper nouns](_oge/pos/PROPN), [pronouns](_oge/pos/PRON),[adjectives](_oge/pos/ADJ), [verbs](_oge/pos/VERB) and very rarely [numerals](_oge/pos/NUM).

### <a name="Sing">`Sing`</a>: singular number

A singular noun denotes one person, animal or thing.

#### Examples

* _ქალაქ_ "city", _სძალ, სძალი_ "daughter-in-law" etc.

### <a name="Plur">`Plur`</a>: plural number

A plural noun denotes several persons, animals or things.

#### Examples

* _ქალაქ<b>ებ</b>ი, _ქალაქ<b>ნ</b>ი_ "cities", _სძლ<b>ებ</b>, სძლ<b>ებ</b>ი, სძალ<b>ნ</b>ი_ "daughter's in law" etc.
