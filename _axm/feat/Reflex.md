Boolean feature of [pronouns](PRON) or
[determiners](DET). It tells whether the word is reflexive,
i.e. refers to the subject of its clause.

In Middle Armenian, the third-person personal pronoun _ինք/inkʿ_ “he/she” also functions as a reflexive pronoun with the exception of the nominative case.

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is reflexive

Note that there is no `No` value. If the word is not reflexive, the `Reflex` feature will just not be mentioned in the `FEAT` 
column (which means that empty value has the `No` meaning.)

#### Examples

* reflexive personal pronouns: _իրեն, զինք, իրմէ, իրմով, զիրենք, իրենցմէ, իրենցմով_ (occur in various cases but not in nominative and distinguish [Number]())
* reflexive possessive determiners: _իր (իրեն), իրենց_
