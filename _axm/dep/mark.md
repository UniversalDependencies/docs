A marker is the subordinating conjunction introducing a finite clause subordinate to another clause.
The `mark` is a dependent of the subordinate clause head.

~~~ sdparse
եւ երբ իր խիստ ողորմութիւն այնէ՝ որ չմեռնի, նա զաչսն հանէ \n and when he exercises his strict mercy so that (the culprit) does not die, then he takes out the eyes
mark(այնէ, երբ)
mark(չմեռնի, որ)
mark(հանէ, նա)
mark(exercises, when)
mark(die, so-that)
mark(takes, then)
~~~

Note that the subordinating conjunction should not be confused with relative pronouns and adverbs.
These fill a valency slot in the frame of the subordinate predicate, and are labeled according to their role in the frame, they are not `mark`:

~~~ sdparse
եւ այս օձս յորժամ ի գնալն լինի, նայ զգլուխն բարձր կենայ \n and when this serpent moves, it holds its head upright
advmod(գնալն, յորժամ)
advmod(moves, when)
~~~
