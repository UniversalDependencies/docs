title: 'det:nummod'
shortdef: 'pronominal quantifier agreeing in case with the noun'
udver: '2'
---

Pronouns that agree with nouns and can be replaced by numerals (_keli, keleri, abu, ”several”,
“several”, “both”_) are
morphologically tagged as [DET](), and their relation is labeled `det:nummod`:

~~~ sdparse
Aš turiu kelis obuolius . \n I have a-few apples .
det:nummod(obuolius, kelis)
det:nummod(apples, a-few)
~~~
