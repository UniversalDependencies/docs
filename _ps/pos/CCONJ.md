### Definition

A coordinating conjunction is a word that links words or larger
constituents (even clauses) without syntactically subordinating one to the other and
expresses a semantic relationship between them.

The coordinating conjunctions usually depend on the head of the second constituent with the [cc]() relation.

For _subordinating conjunctions,_ see [SCONJ]().
