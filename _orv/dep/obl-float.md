The East Slavic floating quantifiers _весь_ 'all', _каждый_ 'every', _одинъ_ 'only; alone', _оба_ 'both', _самъ_ 'oneself' etc. are called “floating” because they are separated from the noun phrase they semantically belong to and can occur in a different syntactic position. The relation `obl:float` is used when a floating quantifier is attached directly to the verb (or other predicative) if the (semantically modified) argument is ommited.  

~~~ sdparse
Владѣемъ своим государством сами . \n We own our state ourselves . 
obl:float(Владѣемъ, сами) 
obl:float(own, ourselves)
~~~

~~~ sdparse
И поидоша псковичи со княземъ великимъ Костянтиномъ во свою землю , вси здрави \n And when the Pskovites went with the Great Prince Kostyantin to their land , all healthy
obl:float(здрави, вси) 
obl:float(healthy, all)
~~~
