The relation `conj:reform` is used in spoken data, when the speaker reformulates an utterance.
The first element is a complete utterance (for incomplete utterances, the relation `reparandum` is used).
The second element is a reformulation of the first.

~~~ sdparse
en fait j' habite dans une maison en banlieue , la banlieue parisienne \n Actually, I live in a house in the suburbs—the Paris suburbs
conj:reform(banlieue-9, banlieue-12)
~~~
