Whenever possible, the oblique relation is specified as locative (spatial) modifier.

- "And on the way the soul is lamneting about the sins it committed."
~~~ sdparse
ud ruwān andar rāh abar wināh ī =š kird hangrāyīdār \n and soul in way over sin REL OBL.3SG did lamenting
case(rāh, andar)
obl:lmod(hangrāyīdār, rāh)
~~~

- "Below the earth, there is water everywhere."
~~~ sdparse
azēr ēn zamīg hamāg gyāg āb bē ēstēd \n below this earth all place water out stands
advmod:lmod(ēstēd, azēr)
det(zamīg, ēn)
nmod(azēr, zamīg)
det(gyāg, hamāg)
obl:lmod(ēstēd, gyāg)
nsubj(ēstēd, āb)
advmod(ēstēd, bē)
~~~
