### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: referential

We use Referential to mark linguistic items with the so-called augment, 
a word-initial vowel that commonly occurs on nominals such as nouns, adjectives, demonstrative pronouns, etc.
In addition, the augment occurs verb-initially in subject relative clauses and can also precede some adverbs.
The use of the augment has been related to referential functions.

#### Examples

* _<b>o</b>muyembe_ "mango"
