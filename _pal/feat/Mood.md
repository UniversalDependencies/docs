This feature takes the following values: Imp, Ind, Nec, Opt, Sub.

Only finite verb forms of the so-called present stem are marked for mood. The members of an analytic formation are annotated individually (e.g. optative particle + verb in the indicative).

### <a name='Imp'>`Imp`: explanation of the value
The imperative mood has two distinct forms for the 2nd person. An order to a 3rd person is expressed by means of the necessitative.<br>The imperative is negated by the prohibitive particle _ma_.

|imperative|singular|plural|
|---|---|---|
|"go!"|_raw_|_rawēd_|

### <a name='Ind'>`Ind`: explanation of the value
The indicative mood is the neutral form of a verb. It can occur in any modal context, but expresses factivity when contrasted with other moods.

|indicative|singular|plural|
|---|---|---|
|1st|_rawam_|_rawēm_|
|2nd|_rawē(h)_|_rawēd_|
|3rd|_rawēd_|_rawēnd_|

### <a name='Nec'>`Nec`: explanation of the value
The necessitative uses the verbal noun in _-išn_ or the long infinitive. It is usually infinite, but the auxiliary verb "to be" can be used to mark verbal categories. The subject of a necessitative is always in oblique case (if formally available).

### <a name='Opt'>`Opt`: explanation of the value
The optative mood expresses a wish. In combination with a past tense form, it expresses the irrealis. Not all forms are attested with certainty.

|indicative|singular|plural|
|---|---|---|
|1st|_rawēm_|_rawēm_|
|2nd|_rawē(h)_ or _rawēš_|_rawēd_|
|3rd|_rawē(h)_|_rawēnd_|

### <a name='Sub'>`Sub`: explanation of the value
The subjunctive mood expresses a possibility.

|indicative|singular|plural|
|---|---|---|
|1st|_rawān_|_rawām_|
|2nd|_rawā(h)_|_rawād_|
|3rd|_rawād_|_rawānd_|

#### Example
- imperative: _kē pad sūdōmand kāmēd būdan <b>gōw</b> kū afsōs <b>ma kun</b>_ "Who wishes to be with (someone) advantageous, tell (him): **Do not mock**!"
- necessitative: _ud andar xešm kēn az menišn ī xwēš abāz <b>dāštan</b>_ "And in anger, (one) **should keep** hate out of one's own thinking!" <br> _man anšahrīg hē u=t anšahrīgīh ī man <b>kunišn</b>_ "(You) are my slave and you (obl.) **have to do** the my slave labour!"
- indicative: _pad ān ummēd <b>dārēd</b> ī <b>sazēd</b> būdan_ "he **has** hopes for what **ought** to be"
- optative: _agar=im pad tīr bē zanē weh <b>sahē</b> kū abar ēn pad gām rawam_ "(Even) if you shoot me with an arrow, it **would seem** better than going on this (bridge) by (one) step!"
- subjunctive: _ud pidar pad ēn kū tā hamāg tan zahr nē rasēd frazand nē <b>mīrād</b> angust ī frazand brīnēd_ "And the father cuts the finger of the child so that the poison does not spread to the whole body (and) the child **would die**."
