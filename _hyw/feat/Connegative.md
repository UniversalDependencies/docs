Boolean feature of verbs ([VERB]() and [AUX]()) marking a word as being in the connegative form.

In Western Armenian, the negated indicative mood is formed with the negative auxiliary verb (*չեմ*) in the present or imperfect tenses and the main verb, which typically appears in the connegative form (traditionally called _negative participle_). The singular negative imperative is likewise formed with the connegative form preceded by the negative particle _մի_/_mi_.

Note that there is no `No` value. If the word is not connegative, the `Connegative` feature will not appear.

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: word is connegative

#### Examples

* _չեմ, չես, չի (չ’), չենք, չէք, չեն <b>գրեր</b>_/_čem, čes, či (č’), čenk’, čēk’, čen <b>grer</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they will not <b>write</b>”,
* _չէի, չէիր, չէր, չէինք, չէիք, չէին <b>գրեր</b>_/_čēi, čēir, čēr, čēink’, čēik’, čēin <b>grer</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they would not <b>write</b>”,
* _չեմ, չես, չի (չ’), չենք, չէք, չեն <b>կարդար</b>_/_čem, čes, či (č’), čenk’, čēk’, čen <b>kardar</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they will not <b>read</b>”,
* _չէի, չէիր, չէր, չէինք, չէիք, չէին <b>կարդար</b>_/_čēi, čēir, čēr, čēink’, čēik’, čēin <b>kardar</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they would not <b>read</b>”,
* _չեմ, չես, չի (չ’), չենք, չէք, չեն <b>խօսիր</b>_/_čem, čes, či (č’), čenk’, čēk’, čen <b>xòsir</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they will not <b>speak</b>”,
* _չէի, չէիր, չէր, չէինք, չէիք, չէին <b>խօսիր</b>_/_čēi, čēir, čēr, čēink’, čēik’, čēin <b>xòsir</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they would not <b>speak</b>”,
* _չեմ, չես, չի (չ’), չենք, չէք, չեն <b>թողուր</b>_/_čem, čes, či (č’), čenk’, čēk’, čen <b>t’oġowr</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they will not <b>allow</b>”,
* _չէի, չէիր, չէր, չէինք, չէիք, չէին <b>թողուր</b>_/_čēi, čēir, čēr, čēink’, čēik’, čēin <b>t’oġowr</b>_ “I, you, he/she/it, we, you, they would not <b>allow</b>”.
* _մի՛ <b>գրեր, կարդար, խօսիր, թողուր</b>_/_mi՛ <b>grer, kardar, xòsir, t’oġowr</b>_ “don't <b>write, read, speak, allow</b>”
