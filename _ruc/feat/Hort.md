### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: hortative

Ruuli has several hortative particles: the most common _ka_ and less common _leka_ and _nka_. 
They are always followed by a subjunctive verb form with the first person singular subject.

#### Examples

* _<b>ka</b> nkole_ "let me do"
