In Cuicatec, this feature applies to [nouns](cux-pos/NOUN).

### <a name="Clf">`Clf`</a>: classifier

Classifiers are nouns that precede a personal name and classify its referent.
The classifier _ta_ "señora" is used before the names of women. The classifier
is attached to the name it classifies with the [clf](cux-dep/clf) relation.

#### Examples

* [cux] _<b>Ta</b> Marian ndatata._ "María es mujer."

### <a name="Relat">`Relat`</a>: relational

Relational nouns are nouns that take a possessor and express a relation to it.
They are used to encode obliques, similar to words such as "top" in English or
"lado" in Spanish, and also extend to meanings such as "with" (_nukut_ "con él").

The most frequent relational noun is the possessive base _ñeʼe_, which is
inflected for its possessor (_ñeʼë_ "de mí", _ñeʼed_ "de ti", _ñeʼe_ "de él",
_ñeʼen_ "de nosotros", _ñeʼey_ "de ellos") and is used to express possession,
for example as the predicate of "X is mine/yours".

#### Examples

* [cux] _Kuetíchi kon <b>ñeʼed</b>._ "Esa canasta es tuya."
* [cux] _Gua yana <b>ñeʼë</b>._ "No tengo perro."
* [cux] _Ká ama it dítuu tumin techkó noʼo <b>nukut</b>._ "Compré una mula para acarrear leña."
