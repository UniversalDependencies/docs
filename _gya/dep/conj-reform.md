The relation `conj:reform` is used in spoken data, when the speaker reformulates an utterance.
The first element is a complete utterance.
The second element is a reformulation of the first.


~~~ sdparse
wà wàntò mɛ́-B H-ɗoŋ wán táá hɛ̀n ɗòŋ mɔ̀ hɛ̰̀ ɗòŋ =ám hɛ̰̀ \n "Well, Wanto, come on over behind—behind me, right here," she said.
conj:reform (mɔ̀,=ám)
~~~
