`Case[sauf]` is an inflectional feature of [nouns](_oge/pos/NOUN), [proper nouns](_oge/pos/PROPN), [pronouns](_oge/pos/PRON), [adjectives](_oge/pos/ADJ), [numerals](_oge/pos/NUM), [verbs](_oge/pos/VERB) and [adpositions](_oge/pos/ADP). In Old Georgian, *Suffixaufnahme* consists of the repetition of case and number endings of the head noun onto other nominals governed by it, and it mainly functions as a strategy for delimiting noun phrase constituency. It is also annotated with [`Number[sauf]`](_oge/feat/Number[sauf]).
Occasionally, *Suffixaufnahme* can be double, resulting in the stacking of three case endings. These are annotated with [`Case[sauf2]`](_oge/feat/Case[sauf2]).

### <a name="Nom">`Nom`</a>: nominative case

Nominative case (singular or plural) stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _სპარსთაჲ_ 'of the Persians' (Gen with Nom)  etc.

### <a name="Erg">`Erg`</a>: ergative case

Ergative case (singular or plural) stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _ზეცისამან_ 'of sky' (Gen with Erg) etc.

### <a name="Gen">`Gen`</a>: genitive case

Genitive case (singular or plural) stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _უფლისაჲსა_ 'of Lord' (Gen with Gen) etc.

### <a name="Dat">`Dat`</a>: dative case

Dative case (singular or plural) stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _უფლისასა_ 'of Lord' (Gen with Dat) etc.

### <a name="Ins">`Ins`</a>: instrumental case

Instrumental case stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _მეფასისაჲთა_ 'of guardian' (Gen with Ins) etc.

### <a name="Ess">`Ess`</a>: adverbial case

Adverbial case stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _ჰურიასტანისად_ 'of Judea' (Gen with Adv) etc.

### <a name="Voc">`Voc`</a>: vocative case

Vocative case (singular or plural) stacked to the genitive case.

#### Examples

* _ისრაელისაო_ 'of Israel' (Gen with Voc) etc.
