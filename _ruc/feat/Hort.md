In Ruuli, certain particles occur only in hortative utterances. These are utterances that express prescriptions where the recipient of the prescription is the second person and the performer of the prescription includes the first (singular or plural) or occasionally the third person.


### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: hortative

Ruuli has several hortative particles: the most common _ka_ and less common _leka_ and _nka_. 
They are always followed by a subjunctive verb form.

#### Examples

* _<b>ka</b> nkole_ "let me do"
