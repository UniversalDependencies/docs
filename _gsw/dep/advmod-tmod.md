---

title: 'advmod:tmod'
shortdef: 'advmod with temporal modality'
udver: '2'
---

The  _advmod:tmod_ relation is used for adverbial modifiers with temporal modality.

~~~ sdparse
Schnall ghüpst . \n jumped quickly .
advmod:tmod(ghüpst, Schnall)
~~~
