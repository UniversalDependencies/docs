`Case[stack2]` is an inflectional feature of [nouns](_oge/pos/NOUN), [proper nouns](_oge/pos/PROPN), [pronouns](_oge/pos/PRON), [adjectives](_oge/pos/ADJ), [numerals](_oge/pos/NUM) and [verbs](_oge/pos/VERB). This feature is associated with triple case stacking in Old Georgian, i.e. forms in which a genitive marker is followed by two additional case markers. The phenomenon represents an extension of secondary case marking (Suffixaufnahme) described under [Case[stack]](_oge/feat/Case[stack]).

Triple case stacking is considerably rarer than ordinary secondary case marking and is mainly attested in Old Georgian texts. As with doubled case forms, its occurrence is related to agreement between a genitive modifier and its head noun.

### <a name="Gen">`Gen`</a>: triple genitive stacking

In Old Georgian, triple case stacking is encountered in the following cases:

* Triple nominative case, represented by repeated genitive and nominative case markers;
* Triple ergative case, represented by repeated genitive markers followed by the ergative marker;
* Triple dative case, represented by repeated genitive markers followed by the dative marker;
* Triple genitive case, represented by repeated doubling of the genitive marker;
* Triple instrumental case, represented by repeated genitive markers followed by the instrumental marker;
* Triple vocative case, represented by repeated genitive markers followed by the vocative marker.

#### Examples

* _ძელისაჲსაჲ_ 'wooden' (Gen with Nom) etc.;
* _ძელისაჲსამან_ 'by the wooden one' (Gen with Erg) etc.;
* _ძელისაჲსასა_ 'to the wooden one' (Gen with Dat) etc.;
* _ძელისაჲსაჲსა_ 'of the wooden one' (Gen with Gen) etc.;
* _ძელისაჲსაჲთა_ 'with the wooden one' (Gen with Inst) etc.;
* _ძელისაჲსაო_ 'O, wooden one' (Gen with Voc) etc.
