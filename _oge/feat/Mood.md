The `Mood` feature applies to [VERB](_ka/pos/VERB).

### <a name="Ind">`Ind`</a>: indicative

The indicative can be considered the default mood. A verb in indicative merely states that something happens, has happened or will
happen, without adding any attitude of the speaker.

#### Examples

* _დავსწერ_ *davscer* 'I write', _დავსწერდ_ *davscerd* 'I wrote', _დავწერო_ *davcero* 'I will write'

### <a name="Sub">`Sub`</a>: subjunctive

The subjunctive (conjunctive) mood is used for actions that are subjective or otherwise uncertain.

#### Examples

* _დავსწერდე_ *davscerde* 'that I might write', _დავწერო_ *davscero* 'that I may write', _დამეწეროს_ *dameceros* 'that I may have it written'

### <a name="Imp">`Imp`</a>: imperative

The speaker uses imperative to order or ask the addressee to do the action of the verb.

#### Examples

* _წერდი_ *cerdi* 'write!', _დაწერენ_ *daceren* 'write later'
