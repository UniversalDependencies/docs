---
layout: relation
title: 'nmod:obl'
shortdef: 'non-agreeing attribute expressed by an adverbial modifier'
udver: '2'
---

When a non-agreeing attribute is expressed by an adverbial modifier, we label it as nmod:obl.

~~~ sdparse
Dabar populiarus darbas namuose . \n Working from-home is popular now .
nmod:obl(darbas, namuose)
nmod:obl(Working, from-home)
~~~
