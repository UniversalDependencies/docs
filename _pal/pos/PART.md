### Definition
Particles are function words that must be associated with another word or phrase to impart meaning and that do not satisfy definitions of other universal parts of speech.

### Examples
- negation: _nē_ "not", prohibitive _ma_
- modality markers: optative particle _ēw_
- aspect markers: perfective _be_ and imperfective _hamē_
- focus marker: _=iz_

### Border cases
The aspect markers _be_ and _hamē_ derive from [adv]()erbs: _be_ from _bē_ "out, away", _hamē_ "continuously". The adverbial interpretation is the preferred option in case of doubt.
<br>If _=iz_ is used as additive marker "also", we take it as an adverb.
