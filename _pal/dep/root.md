The root of a sentence is typically a finite verb or the nominal predicate. Middle Persian sentences can consist solely of the root.

- "Which one appears more useful to you."
~~~ sdparse
kadār =tān sūdōmandtar sahēd ROOT \n which you.OBL more_useful seems ROOT
nsubj(sahēd, kadār)
xcomp(sahēd, sūdōmandtar)
obl(sahēd, =tān)
root(ROOT, sahēd)
~~~

- "(Herewith it) ends."
~~~ sdparse
frazaft ROOT \n ended ROOT
root(ROOT, frazaft)
~~~
