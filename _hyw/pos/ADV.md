### Definition

Adverbs are words that typically modify [verbs](VERB) for such categories as time, place, direction or manner. They may also modify
[adjectives](ADJ) and other adverbs, as in _**հաւանաբար** սխալ_/_**hawanapar** skhal_ “probably wrong” or _**շատ** աղուոր_/_**shad** aghuor_ “very nice”. 

Some complex adverbs are fixed multiword expressions, e.g. _երբ եւ իցէ_/_erp ew itsʿē_ “ever, whenever”, _արդէն իսկ_/_artēn isg_ “already”; _քիչ մը_/_kʿichʿ mě_ “a little”

There is a closed subclass of _pronominal adverbs_ that refer to circumstances in context, rather than naming them directly; similarly
to pronouns, these can be categorized as interrogative, relative, demonstrative, indefinite. Pronominal adverbs also get the `ADV` part-of-speech tag but they are differentiated by additional features. They inflect for [PronType](), and 
demonstrative adverbs additionally inflect for [Deixis]().

Note that in Western Armenian some adverbs can form comparative and superlative [Degree](). The second degree is expressed by the adverb _**աւելի**_/_**aveli**_ “more” e.g. _**աւելի** վեր_/_**aweli** ver_ “higher, further up”. The third degree of adverbs
in Western Armenian is constructed with the feature of [Definite](), e.g. _ամէնաշատը_/_amēnashadě_ “the most”.

### Examples

- _շատ_/_shad_ “very”
- _լավ_/_lav_ “well”
- _հաւանաբար_/_hawanapar_ “probably”
- _վաղը_/_vaghě_ “tomorrow”
- _վեր_/_ver_, _վար_/_var_ “up, down”
- interrogative adverbs: _ուր_/_ur_  “where”, _երբ_/_erp_  “when”; _ինչպես_/_inchʿbes_ “how”; _ինչու_/_inchʿu_ “why”; _որքան_/_orkʿan_, _որչափ_/_orchʿapʿ_ “how much”
- demonstrative adverbs: _հոս_/_hos_ “here”; _հոն_/_hon_ “there”; _այսպես_/_aysbes_ “this way”, _այնպես_/_aynbes_ “that way”, _այսչափ_/_ayschʿapʿ_ “this much”, _այնչափ_/_aynchʿapʿ_ “so much”
- indefinite adverbs: _երբևէ_/_erbewē_ “ever, whenever”
