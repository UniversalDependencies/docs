### Definition
A numeral is a word, functioning most typically as a [determiner](DET), [adjective](ADJ) or [pronoun](PRON), that expresses a number and a relation to the number, such as quantity, sequence, frequency or fraction.

[Cardinal](NumType) numbers are always covered by `NUM`, while the part-of-speech of [ordinal](NumType) numbers depends on their syntactic embedding.

Numerals can be written as a word, e.g. _ēk_ "one" written ʾywk, or _se_ "three" written with the Aramaeogram tltʾ. Mostly, numerals are displayed by letters that have a numerical value.

### Examples
- _ēk_ "one", also written _b_
- _dō_ "two", also written _yb_
- panǰāh "fifty", also written _sk_ or _sd_
