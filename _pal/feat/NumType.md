This feature takes the following values: Card, Frac, Mult, Ord, Sets.

### <a name='Card'>`Card`: explanation of the value
Cardinal numbers can appear _pro nomen_ and as quantifiers. They are rarely used as listing devices, where they are similar to ordinal numbers. In rare cases, the numeral follows the noun.
- _ān <b>se</b> šab_ "those **three** nights
- _harw <b>se</b> andar dām meh_ "all **three** (are) greatest among the creatues"
- _aṣ̌əmvohū <b>se</b> guftan_ "The (prayer) Aṣ̌əmvohū is to be said **three** (times)."
- _mard <b>se</b>_ "of men **three**"

### <a name='Frac'>`Frac`: explanation of the value
Fraction is only used for a word that lexically expresses a fraction.
- _ān ī abartom <b>srišwadag</b> xwānīhēd garōdmān_ "The highest **third** is called the Garōdmān paradise."

The elements of analytic expressions are analysed individually as cardinal numbers:
- _nazdist ō <b>se ēk</b> ī ēn zamīg dudīgar ō <b>panǰ ēk</b> ī zamīg_ "first to **one third** of this earth, second to **one fifth** of the earth" (lit.: one of three, one of five)

### <a name='Mult'>`Mult`: explanation of the value
Multiplicative is only used for a word that lexically expresses a multiplicative numeral, e.g. _ēwbār_ "once".

### <a name='Ord'>`Ord`: explanation of the value
Ordinal numbers are derived from cardinal numbers by the suffix _-om_. The first four numbers show irregular or suppletive forms.
- "first": _fradom_, _nazdist_ (nearest), _naxwist_ (earliest)
- "second": _dōwom_, _dudīgar_, _dudom_
- "third": _seyom_, _sidīgar_
-  "fourth": _čahārom_, _tasom_

### <a name='Sets'>`Sets`: explanation of the value
If a word meaning "pair" is used as a quantifier, e.g. _ǰuxt_.
