In Chintang, the (di)transitive [VERB]() overtly marks [Number]() and [Person]() agreement with its agents and patients. The layers corresponding to agent agreement are annotated as default ones (`Number` and `Person`). The patient agreement is annotated on the `Number[p]` and `Person[p]` layers.

### <a name="Sing">`Sing`</a>: singular

#### Examples

* *nakhage* "they saw you (singular)"

### <a name="Dual">`Dual`</a>: dual

#### Examples

* *nakhagace* "they saw you (two)"

### <a name="Plur">`Plur`</a>: plural

#### Examples

* *nakhagihẽ* "they saw you (more than two)"
