This feature takes the following values: Conv, Fin, Inf, Part, Vnoun.

### <a name='Conv'>`Conv`: Converb
`Participals` (see below) can be used as converbs in Middle Persian, notably the resultative participle with an anterior reading. Since the same form can also be read as a finite verb form, the analysis relies on contextual information or peculiar word ordering.

- "<b>Plunged back into darkness</b>, (Ahreman) miscreated many demons."
~~~ sdparse
abāz ō tom dwārist kirrēnīd was dēw \n back to darkness rushed miscreated many demon
advmod(tom, abāz)
case(tom, ō)
advcl(kirrēnīd, dwārist)
det(dēw, was)
obj(kirrēnīd, dēw)
~~~

### <a name='Fin'>`Fin`: Finite
Finite verb forms are marked for [person](Person), [number](Number) and [mood](Mood). They head a clause or sentence.

### <a name='Inf'>`Inf`: Infinite
Infinite verb forms are not marked at all. They can be interpreted as active or passive. As a [necessitative](Mood), they can head a clause or sentence, as dependent verbs they can head a subclause. They can convert to nouns.<br>There is a long and a short infinitive in Middle Persian. The long one continues the Old Persian infinitive in _-tanai_, the short one continues deverbal abstract nouns in _-ti-_. So far, it was not possible to identify a semantic difference. The short infinitive seems to be preferred in modal constructions.
- short infinitive: _raft_
- long infinitive: _raftan_

### <a name='Part'>`Part`: Participle
Participial forms are marked for aspect, which we call [tense](Tense) for traditional reasons. They are used in [analytic verb](aux_) forms (e.g. the perfect). On their own, they can head a converbial clause. They can convert to [adjectives](ADJ) and [nouns](NOUN).
- so-called present participle: _rawān_
- so-called past participle: _raft_

### <a name='Vnoun'>`Vnoun`: Verbal noun
The verbal noun in _-išn_ can be used as a `necessitative`. It can be interpreted as active or passive. As a necessitative, they can head a clause or sentence. It can convert to a `noun` (_nomen actionis_).
- _rawišn_
