### <a name="Neg">`Neg`</a>: Negated
Only one word form is inherently marked as negative. Polarity is not indicated for nominals (such as privative prefix _a(n)-_ or derivations with _abē_ "without, -less").
#### Examples 
- _[nēst](VerbType)_ "is not" (an amalgamation of Old Persian _nai asti_).
