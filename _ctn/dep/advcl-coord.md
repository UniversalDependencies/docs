The dependent of an `advcl:coord` relation is a counterfactual comparison adverbial clause, which is headed by a coordinated action converb ([VerbForm]()=`Cnv`, [ConvType]()=`Coord`).

~~~ sdparse
Khusaŋa ta thaptummehẽ . \n Carry.CVB.COORD FOC we_brought_him_over .
advcl:coord(thaptummehẽ, Khusaŋa)
advcl:coord(we_brought_him_over, Carry.CVB.COORD)
~~~
