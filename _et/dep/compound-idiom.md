Nominal components of phraseological verbs (`compound:idiom`) are used in connection with *phraseological verbs*, (väljendverb in Estonian)  where the noun is considered an integral part of the verb expression. The governor of the dependency is the verb, and the dependent is the noun.

~~~ sdparse
Nad pöörasid omavahel tülli . 
compound:idiom(pöörasid, tülli)
~~~
