In Ruuli, possessive pronouns agree both with the possessor and the possessee. 
_Person[psed]_ reflects the person value of the possessee.
It is realized by the initial morpheme of the possessive pronoun. 

### <a name="3">`3`</a>: third

Agreement with the possessee is realized as a paradigm of third person markers, known as noun classes.
The finer-grained value of this marker is annotated as _NounClass[psed]_.

#### Examples

* _omwana <b>wa</b>iswe_ "our child" (agreement of noun class 1)
* _amaizi <b>ga</b>ange_ "my water" (agreement of noun class 6)
