### <a name='Yes'>`Yes`: Possessive
The reflexive [pronoun](PRON) _xwēš_ "own" usually denotes a possessive relation of the topic of the clause and the respective head of the reflexive pronoun. Then it is marked as possessive. Orthotone personal pronouns are marked as possessive if they appear in the determiner position, but not, if they appear in the position of a nominal modifier (i.e. in an _ezāfe_ construction).
- possessive _xwēš_: <br/>_mard ē kē duxt <b>ī xwēš</b> rāy gōwēd_ "a man says about **his** daughter"<br/>_čē=t pad frazām abestām ō kunišn <b>ī xwēš</b> bawēd_ "because in the end, you will find support in **your** deeds"<br/>_ī kadagxwadāy andar <b>xwēš</b> zīwandagīh sālārīh ī dūdag ī hudādestān awi=š kunēd_ "to whom the paterfamilias appoints the guardianship of the family according to good judgement during **his** lifetime"
- personal pronoun as possessive determiner: _čē=m andar <b>ašmāh</b> kām kunišn man āgāhēnīd framāyēd pad yazdān kām_ "Please (lit. deign to) inform (me) by the will of the gods what I am supposed to do according to **your** wish!"
- personal pronoun as nominal modifier: _ardaxšīr abāg dō bārag <b>ī ašmāh</b> nē pad gyāg ast_ "Ardaxšīr and (lit. with) two horses of **yours** are not at place (i.e. are gone)."
