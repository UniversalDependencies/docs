title: 'nsubj:instrument'
shortdef: 'nominal subject of an instrument-voice verb'
udver: '2'
---

`nsubj:instrument` is used for the nominal subject of a clause in instrument 
voice. It is signalled as such by word order (pre-verbal position) and/or 
marking with a pivot-marking adposition.

~~~ sdparse
Bulonggo pilotubu'u uponula \n pan UV2.rea-cook-1SG fish
nsubj:instrument(pilotubu'u, Bulonggo)
nsubj:instrument(UV2.rea-cook-1SG, pan)
~~~

“\[I\] cook fish with the pan.” (instrument voice)
