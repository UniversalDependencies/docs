In Cuicatec, this feature applies to [verbs](cux-pos/VERB). Third-person agreement on the
verb has distinct forms depending on the subject: besides masculine and feminine forms
(see [Gender](u-feat/Gender)), there is a form for human subjects in general and a form
for animal subjects.

### <a name="Hum">`Hum`</a>: human

The third-person subject is human. The verb typically ends in _-y_ or _-ñ_.

#### Examples

* [cux] _Iña ñeʼe noba <b>jeʼey</b> tíku._ "Los oaxaqueños comen chapulines."
* [cux] _Diya <b>chinuñ</b> ama chibatu._ "Mi tío mató un chivo."

### <a name="Aml">`Aml`</a>: animal

The third-person subject is an animal. The verb typically ends in _-t_.

#### Examples

* [cux] _Leeko <b>jeʼet</b> iti._ "El coyote come animales."
* [cux] _Tuʼu <b>cheʼet</b> kulantro._ "El pollo se comió el cilantro."
