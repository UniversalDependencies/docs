This covers a range of compound reduplicated forms or echo words in Middle Armenian. The reduplicative and echo words get the same POS tag as the head word or will be tagged according to their basic use.

See also the related feature [Hyph]().

### <a name="Rdp">`Rdp`</a>: reduplicative

The word is a copy of a previous word. Тhis would add the meaning of distribution, separation, variety, diversity or just emphasis.

#### Examples

* _ատեն <b>ատեն</b>/aten aten_ “from time to time”,
* _կաթ <b>կաթ</b>/kat’ kat’_ “drop by drop”,
* _հանկուստ <b>հանկուստ</b>/hankowst hankowst_ “knotted, having knots”,
* _հատ <b>հատ</b>/hat hat_ “one by one”,
* _շիթ <b>շիթ</b>/šit’ šit’_ “in jets, jet by jet”,
* _ծվէն <b>ծվէն</b>/çvēn çvēn_ “in shreds, in tatters”

In some cases one of the words in reduplicated forms may also be modified individually by other words.

* _եռ ընդ <b>եռ</b>/eṙ ënd eṙ_ “boiling hot”,
* _գող ի <b>գող</b>/goġ i goġ_ “stealthily”

### <a name="Ech">`Ech`</a>: echo

The word fully or partly rhymes with a previous or following word but it is not identical to it and typically it does not have any meaning of its own. 
The echoed part is a form that does not occur elsewhere than in compounds. In Middle Armenian it generalizes or specifies the meaning of the previous or following word and is translated
as “or something”, “etc.”, “like that” etc.

#### Examples

* _աքիս <b>մաքիս</b>/ak’is mak’is_ “like a weasel, as a weasel”,
* _աղուշ <b>մաղուշ</b>/aġowš maġowš_ “sweetly, in a sweet and gentle way”,
* _<b>հառոք</b> և փառոք/haṙok’ ew p’aṙok’_ “good and proper”,
* _սուտ ու <b>փութ</b>/sowt ow p’owt’_ “false, pretended”,
* _<b>աժնել</b> բաժնել/ažnel bažnel_ “separate from each other”,
