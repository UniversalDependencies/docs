### Definition

Adverbs are words that typically modify [verbs](VERB) for such categories as time, place, direction or manner. They may also modify [adjectives](ADJ) and other adverbs, as in _<b>հաւանաբար</b> սխալ_/_<b>hawanabar</b> sxal_ “probably wrong” or _<b>շատ</b> աղուոր_/_<b>šat</b> aġowor_ “very nice”. 

Some complex adverbs are fixed multiword expressions, e.g. _երբ եւ իցէ_/_erb ew ic’ē_ “ever”, _արդէն իսկ_/_ardēn isk_ “already”; _քիչ մը_/_k’ič më_ “a little”.

There is a closed subclass of _pronominal adverbs_ that refer to circumstances in context, rather than naming them directly; similarly to pronouns, these can be categorized as interrogative, relative, demonstrative, indefinite. Pronominal adverbs also get the `ADV` part-of-speech tag but they are differentiated by additional features. They bear the feature [PronType](), and demonstrative adverbs additionally bear the feature [Deixis]().

Note that in Western Armenian some adverbs can form comparative and superlative [Degree](). The comparative degree is expressed by the adverb _<b>աւելի</b>_/_<b>aweli</b>_ “more” e.g. _<b>աւելի</b> վեր_/_<b>aweli</b> ver_ “higher, further up”. The superlative degree is typically formed with the prefix _ամենա-_/_amena-_, or the suffix _-գույն_/_-gowyn_ “most” and often contains the definite article. However, the article is not analyzed separately and is not reflected in the annotation, e.g. _ամէնաշատը_/_amēnashadě_ “the most”.

### Examples

- _շատ_/_šat_ “very”
- _լավ_/_lav_ “well”
- _հաւանաբար_/_hawanabar_ “probably”
- _վաղը_/_vaġë_ “tomorrow”
- _վեր_/_ver_, _վար_/_var_ “up, down”
- interrogative and relative adverbs: _ուր_/_owr_  “where”, _երբ_/_erb_  “when”, _ինչպես_/_inčpes_ “how”, _ինչու_/_inčow_ “why”, _որքան_/_ork’an_, _որչափ_/_orčap’_ “how much”
- demonstrative adverbs: _հոս_/_hos_ “here”, _հոն_/_hon_ “there”, _այսպես_/_ayspes_ “this way”, _այնպես_/_aynpes_ “that way”, _այսչափ_/_aysčap’_ “this much”, _այնչափ_/_aynčap’_ “so much”
- indefinite adverbs: _երբևէ_/_erbewē_ “ever”
