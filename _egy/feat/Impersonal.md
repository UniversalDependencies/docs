Two impersonal constructions are found in Earlier Egyptian: The "man"-impersonal construction, for example "man kauft ein Auto" in German and the "third-person" impersonal construction, for example "vinieron a trabajar" in Spanish.

### <a name="Man">`Man`</a>: Man-impersonal construction

The noun tꞽ/tw is used as an impersonal subject ("one" in English, "man" in German).
