### Definition

Megrelian particles, can be represented by a suffix attached to an inflected word (e.g. _<b>ვა</b>ჭარა_vɑt͡ʃʼɑrɑ_ 'do not write' etc.), or by an independent word (e.g. _ხოლო_xɔlɔ_ 'too'). Particles are subdivided into the following lexical classes: interrogative (PartType=Int), affirmative (PartType=Ta), negative (PartType=Neg), modal (PartType=Tm), discourse (PartType=Disc) and.
