Mehweb gender (class) agreement distinguishes masculine, two feminine and neuter in the singular and human and non-human in the plural. Morphologically singular mass nouns control non-human plural agreement.  
The distribution of two feminine markers is roughly between married/old (Fem) and unmarried/young (F1) women.  
Verbs and adjectives have gender markers in the prefix position, and not all verbs have this slot. Locative nominal forms and auxiliaries have a suffix slot.  

### <a name="Masc">`Masc`</a>: masculine gender

#### Examples

* _madina-s <b>rasul</b> <b>w</b>-ig-uwe le-<b>w</b>_ : Madina-DAT Rasul(ABS) <b>M</b>-love:IPF-CVB.IPFV AUX-<b>M</b> "Madina loves _Rasul_."

### <a name="Fem">`Fem`</a>: feminine gender

The marker for feminine -d-r- is identical to non-human plural marker.  

#### Examples

* _rasuj-ze b-ah-ur abaj iz-uwe le-<b>r</b>-deš ile_ : Rasul-INTER(LAT) N-know:PF-AOR mother(ABS) be.sick:IPF-CVB.IPFV AUX-<b>F</b>-NMLZ COMP "Rasul found out that his _mother_ was sick."

### <a name="F1">`F1`</a>: feminine F1 gender

An 'old' marker for feminine -r- is preserved to show agreement with nouns denoting unmarried/young (F1) women.

#### Examples

* _rasuj-s <b>madina</b> <b>d</b>-ig-uwe le-r_ : Rasul+OBL-DAT Madina(ABS) F1-love:IPF-CVB.IPFV AUX-F "Rasul loves _Madina_."


### <a name="Neut">`Neut`</a>: neuter gender

If a clause lacks an absolutive argument, as observed with some types of for- mally transitive verbs, gender agreement on the lexical verb appears as the de- fault singular neuter agreement marker b-. This is also observed with intransitive impersonal predicates.

#### Examples

* _doˤʜi ar-<b>b</b>-ik-ib_ : snow(ABS) PV-<b>N</b>-fall:PFV-AOR "The _snow_ fell."


### <a name="Hum">`Hum`</a>: human plural agreement class 

#### Examples

* _nuša ustuj-šu-b ka-<b>b</b>-iʔ-i-ra_ : we table.OBL-AD-HPL(ESS) PV-<b>HPL</b>-sit:PFV-AOR-EGO "_We_ are sitting near the table."

### <a name="Nhum">`Nhum`</a>: non-human plural agreement class  

#### Examples

* _it-ini mura <b>d</b>-iχ-ib_ : this-ERG hay <b>NPL</b>-bring:IPFV-IPFT "He was bringing _hay_."
