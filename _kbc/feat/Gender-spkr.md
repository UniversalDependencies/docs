# Gender[spkr]

This Kadiwéu-specific subtype of `Gender` marks the speaker gender encoded by a first-person singular personal pronoun. It describes the speaker, not the referent.

`Masc`: masculine-speaker form *ee* ‘I’.

`Fem`: feminine-speaker form *eeyo* ‘I’.

Griffiths (2002: 40–41) identifies *ee* as the masculine form and *eeyo* as the feminine form. Annotate the pronoun with `Gender[spkr]=Masc` or `Gender[spkr]=Fem`, respectively. Do not use this subtype for ordinary nominal gender, which is annotated with `Gender`.
