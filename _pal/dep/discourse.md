The discourse relation is used for [interjections](INTJ) and [vocative](PART) particles.

- "(She) replied to him: O evil one! I am your Dēn, who (is) your own actions." (i.e. the personification of his actions in life)
~~~ sdparse
passox ō ōy gōwēd kū ay druwand man dēn ī tō kē =t xwēš kunišn ham \n reply to 3SG says that o evil I religion EZ you REL your own action am
discourse(druwand, ay)
vocative(dēn, druwand)
nsubj(dēn, man)
cop(dēn, ham)
~~~
