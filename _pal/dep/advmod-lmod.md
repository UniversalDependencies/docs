Adverbial modifiers can be specified as locative. This also includes preverbs.

- "and a foul wind from the north is blowing towards (it)"
~~~ sdparse
ud gandag wād ī abāxtarīgīhā bē ō padīrag āyēd \n and foul wind EZ northern forth to against comes
case(padīrag, ō)
advmod:lmod(āyēd, padīrag)

- "and seat it (= the soul) on the richly decorated throne"
~~~ sdparse
u =š pad gāh ī harwisppēsīd abar nišānēd \n and it in throne EZ all_adorned upon seat.IMP.PL
advmod:lmod(nišānēd, abar)
~~~
