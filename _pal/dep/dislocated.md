Dislocation is a frequent relation in Middle Persian texts. Early annotations used `dislocated` but we now differentiate between dislocation of topics ([dislocated:topic]()) vs. placeholders or resumptive pronouns ([dislocated:res]()).

- "And (now to) the woman; one's own Pādixšāy(-woman) and the Čagar(-woman) and the ...(?) except for the none-Pādixšāy(-women) are not to be employed as maidservants."

~~~ sdparse
ud zan ān xwēš pāduxšāyīhā ud čagar ud X pad paristār bē ān ī nāpāduxšāyīhā nē dād \n and woman that own of Pādixšāy (marriage) status and person of the Čagar (marriage) status and as maidservant except that ezafe not of the status of Pādixšāy marriage not appoint
cc(dād, ud-1)
dislocated(dād, zan)
~~~
