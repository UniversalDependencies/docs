This feature takes the following values: Abs, Cmp, Sup.

The positive is not marked and can be used for all degrees. It is not indicated in the annotation. Analytic marking of grades is possible, notably by means of _wēš_ "more"

### <a name='Abs'>`Abs`: Absolute superlative / elative
A stylistic peculiarity is the combination of the comparative suffix and the superlative suffix forming an elative or absolute superlative, e.g. _frāz-dom-tar_ "even more advanced, single most advanced".

### <a name='Cmp'>`Cmp`: Comparative
Comparative degree, which can also express superlative degree, e.g. _frāz-dar_ "further, more advanced". If used without comparison, it evokes the notion of 'to a higher degree than normal'.

### <a name='Sup'>`Sup`: Superlative
Superlative degree, e.g. _frāz-dom_ "furthest, most advanced".

#### Example
- analytic: _ud aburnāyīg andar aškomb mādar <b>wēš tabāh</b> bawēd_ "and the child in the womb of the mother becomes **much injured** (lit: more ruined)"
- comparative: _xwaršēd az māh <b>rōšntar</b>_ "The sun (is) **brighter** than the moon."
- comparative with implicit comparison: _dō hēnd tis <b>garānmīgdar</b> dārišn ēk driyōš ī rāst dudīgar pādišxā ī nihānīgburd_ "Two things are **particularly worthy** of appreciation: one (is) the true poor one, the other (is) the modest powerful one"
- comparative for superlative: _ud rāmišn ī <b>az harw rāmišn frāztar</b> tandrustīh ud abēbīmīh ud husrawīh ud ahlāyīh_ "The comfortable feeling which (is) **most advanced**  of all comfortable feelings (are) physical health and freedom of worry and good reputation and righteousness."
- superlative: _pad kirdan harw kār <b>mādagwarīhātom</b> tis tuxšāgīh_ "As for doing (something), the **most fundamental** aspect of every deed (is) diligence."
