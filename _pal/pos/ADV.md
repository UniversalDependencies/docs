### Definition
Adverbs in the literal sense  modify the semantics of a [VERB]() (traditionally called 'preverbs') or express the manner of a verb. Sentential adverbs modify the whole clause (manner, space, time). This class of part-of-speech is also used for words modifying adjectives ([ADJ]()) or other adverbs. Under certain conditions, adverbs can also modify nouns ([NOUN]()).

A word can be marked as an adverb by the suffix _-īhā_, which happens to be homophonous with the collective suffix.

adverbial:
- _u =m <b>xūbīhā</b> awiš guft_ "and I have replied (lit. said) to him well"
- _awi=š <b>pardazišnīhā</b> hammōxtan_ "to teach (it) exhaustively to him"

preverbial:
- _agar <b>abar</b> nē gīrēd wināh bawēd ayāb nē_ "If (he) does not lift (lit. take **up**) (the corpse), would (it) be a sin or not?"
- _hamēstārīh <b>bē</b> barān az spennāgmēnōy dāmān_ "(I) shall remove (lit. carry **out**) the opposition from the creations of the Bountiful Spirit"
   
sentential
- _se hēnd kē dōst <b>wēš</b> sazēd būd dānāg buzešk nēk ud zan ī xwēš ī nēk_ "(It is) three with whom (it is) **especially** appropriate to be friendly with: the wise, a good doctor, and one's own good wife."
- _petītīgbūd rāy mādagwar tis ēn kū wināh nē kunēd <b>xwēškāmīhā</b>_ "For being repentant, the essential aspect (is) this that (one) does not commit a sin **willfully**."

modifying adjectives:
- _<b>abēr</b> šād_ "very happy"
- _<b>akanārag</b> rāmišntar_ "infinitely more peaceful"

modifying adverbs:
- _<b>was</b> frēftārīhā_ "very deceitfully"
- _<b>abēr</b> was_ "very much"

modifying nouns:
- _husrawīh <b>ēdar</b>_ "the good reputation here (i.e. in this life)"
- _gāh <b>ānōh</b>_ "a place there"

modifying prepositions (annotated as if modifying the noun, see [case]()):
- _gannāgmēnōy <b>abar</b> ō dām ī man ayārīh bar_ "Foul Spirit, offer support to (lit. above to) my creation!"
- _hazār tāg <b>bē</b> az ān draxt bē rust_ "thousand twigs sprout from (lit. out from) that tree"

Deverbal nouns can inherit the respective adverbial from the verb, which is linked like an adjectival attribute to the head in an _ezāfe_ construction (see [advmod]()):
- _ud ka=m <b>hamǰādagīhā</b> nibēsišnīh dīd_ "and when I saw the jumbled writing" (lit. the writing-ness in a cobbled-together manner)"
- _ud was xwāhišn ī <b>lābakkarīhā</b> xwāhēd_ "and (the soul) requests many suppliant requests (lit. requests in a suppliant manner)"

### Examples
- _abēr_ "very"
- _xūb(īhā)_ "well"
- _hamgōnag_ "exactly, likewise"
- _abar_ "above, up"
- _frōd_ "down"
- _čiyōn_ "how"
- _ēdar_ "here"
- _nūn_ "now"
- _fradāg_ "tomorrow"
- _hamēšag_ "always"
- _hagriz_ "never"
- _=iz_ "also, even, and"

### Border cases
Adjectives can be used as adverbs without morphological derivation. Adverbs can appear like postpositions if following a prepositional phrase.
Interrogatives habe been annotated as pronouns (see [PRON]()).
