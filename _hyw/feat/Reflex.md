Boolean feature of [pronouns](PRON) or [determiners](DET). It tells whether the word is reflexive, i.e. refers to the subject of its clause.

Reflexive object of a verb means that the object is the same entity as the subject:

- _կը պատմէ <b>իր</b> մասին_/_kë patmē <b>ir</b> masin_ “he tells about himself” vs.
- _կը պատմէ <b>անոր</b> մասին_/_kë patmē <b>anor</b> masin_ “he tells about him [someone else]”

Reflexive possessives indicate that the subject of the clause is the possessor:

- _<b>իրենց</b> բողոքը կ՚արտայայտեն_/_<b>irencʼ</b> boġokʼë k՚artayayten_ “They express their [own] protest”
- _<b>անոնց</b> բողոքը կ՚արտայայտեն_/_<b>anoncʼ</b> boġokʼë k՚artayayten_ “They express their [someone else] protest”

### <a name="Yes">`Yes`</a>: it is reflexive

Note that there is no `No` value. If the word is not reflexive, the `Reflex` feature will just not be mentioned in the `FEAT` column, which means that empty value has the `No` meaning.

#### Examples

* reflexive personal pronouns: _իր, իրեն_/_ir, iren_ “himself/herself/itself”, _իրենց_/_irencʼ_“themselves” (occur in various cases but not in nominative and distinguish [Number]())
* reflexive possessive determiners: _իր_/_ir_ “his/her/its own”, _իրենց_/_irencʼ_ “their own”
