### Definition

Adverbs are words that typically modify [verbs](_xmf/pos/VERB) for such categories as time, place, direction or manner. In Megrelian, they may also modify [adjectives](_xmf/pos/ADJ) (as in _<b>ძან//ძაან</b> სკვამ_d͡zɑn//d͡zɑɑn skʼvɑm_ "very beautiful"), other adverbs (as in _<b>ძან//ძაან</b> მანგარ_d͡zɑn//d͡zɑɑn</b> mɑnɡɑr_ "very well"). There are eight different types of adverbs in Georgian: local (AdvType=Loc), temporal (AdvType=Tim), modifier (AdvType=Man), causal (AdvType=Cau), interrogative (AdvType=Int), relative (AdvType=Rel) and directional (AdvType=Dir). Generally, adverbs can be considered a closed class of items which can be expanded with two slots, namely a slot for postpositions and for indirect speech markers, but in Georgian, adverbs often have particles added to them, indicating their type, see [PartType](_xmf/feat/PartType).

- Mod, Spec (manner adverb): AdvType=Man
- Caus (causal adverb): AdvType=Cau
- Q (interrogative adverb): AdvType=Int
- Rel1 (relative adverb): AdvType=Rel
- Dir (directional adverb): AdvType=Dir

### Examples

- local adverbs: _სო_sɔ_ 'where' etc.
- temporal adverbs: _მუდოს_mudɔs_ 'when' etc.
- modifier adverbs: _ძან//ძაან_d͡zɑn//d͡zɑɑn_ 'very' etc.
- causal adverbs: _წორეთ_t͡sʼɔrɛt_ 'exactly' etc.
- manner adverbs: _ჭიე-ჭიეთ//ჭიჭე-ჭიჭეთ_t͡ʃʼiɛ-t͡ʃʼiɛt//t͡ʃʼit͡ʃʼɛ-t͡ʃʼit͡ʃʼɛt_ 'little by little' etc.
- interrogative adverbs: _მუსხიი_musxii_ 'how many' etc.
- relative adverbs: _სოთ_sɔt_ 'where' etc.
