In Kadiwéu, nouns and pronouns may distinguish the speaker’s gender. The feature describes the speaker, not the gender of the word’s referent.

### <a name="Fem">`Fem`</a>: feminine speaker

#### Examples

* [kbc] _eeyo_ ‘I’ `Gender[spkr]=Fem`

### <a name="Masc">`Masc`</a>: masculine speaker

#### Examples

* [kbc] _ee_ ‘I’ `Gender[spkr]=Masc`

Griffiths (2002: 40–41) identifies *eeyo* as the form used by feminine speakers and *ee* as the form used by masculine speakers (entries **eeyo** and **ee**). This feature is distinct from `Gender`, which marks nominal gender.

## References

* Griffiths, Glyn. 2002. _Dicionário da língua Kadiwéu_. Sociedade Internacional de Linguística.
