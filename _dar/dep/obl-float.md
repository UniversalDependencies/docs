title: 'obl:float'
shortdef: 'oblique as a floating modifier'
udver: '2'
---

The relation `obl:float` is used for floating modifiers with the meaning 'all', 'every', 'each', 'only', 'alone', 'both', 'oneself' (emphatic) etc. that modify nominal arguments or the predicate. 

~~~ sdparse
nuni ħuša k'ʷijalla χʷasar baq'iša caca ʁurušize . \n I will rescue the two of you for one rouble each . 
obl:float(ʁurušize, caca) 
obl:float(rouble, each)
~~~

gloss: I-ERG you.pl two-ORD-ADD rescue HPL-do:PFV-FUT.EGO one-one rouble-INTER(LAT)

`obl:float` can attach directly to the verb (or other predicative) if the (semantically modified) argument is ommited.
