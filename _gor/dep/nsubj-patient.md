title: 'nsubj:patient'
shortdef: 'patient subject of a patient voice verb'
udver: '2'
---

A nominal argument of a clause that is in the non-pivot role, but is semantically the 
agent of the clause. This occurs when a clause is in patient voice 
or another voice other than actor voice.
`nsubj:patient` replaces `nsubj:pass` in our label set, as the patient voice in 
symmetrical voice languages is very different in characteristics and function to the 
passive voice in Indo-European languages.

~~~ sdparse
Oile healipo li Dewi lo pito \n mango peeling NPIV Dewi NPIV knife
obj:agent(healipo, Dewi)
obj:agent(peeling, Dewi)
~~~

“Dewi is peeling the mango with a knife.” (instrument voice)
