In French Sign Language, for reported speech, the quoted speech may contain with a mention of the addressee.
Speaker is analysed with a relation `discourse:participant`.


~~~ sdparse
[hep] [MAMAN] [OUI] [DIFFÉRENT] [DONNER] [DÉCOUVERTE] [DIFFÉRENT] [RICHE] [INTÉRESSANT] \n Mom finished (saying): "With the different places, the discoveries where diverse and riche. It was very interesting,
discourse:participant ([DÉCOUVERTE], [MAMAN])
~~~
