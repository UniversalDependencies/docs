Gender of the applicative suffix of a verb. Tigrinya verbs can take an applicative suffix with the element -l- (-ለ-, -ሉ, -ላ, …), which cross-references an applied argument such as a beneficiary, maleficiary, instrument, source or location. The applicative suffixes form a series distinct from the ordinary object suffixes, whose features are recorded by [Gender[obj]](https://universaldependencies.org/ti/feat/Gender-obj.html). The feature records the gender of the applicative suffix, independently of the gender of the subject (`Gender`). The *l* of the existential verb ኣለ / *ʔalə* and its negative የለ / *yələ* belongs to the stem, so their suffixes are object suffixes (`[obj]`), not applicative ones.

### <a name="Masc">`Masc`</a>: masculine applied argument

#### Examples

* ገዚአሉ / *gəziʔɛlu* "I bought (it) for him" (beneficiary): `Person=1|Gender[app]=Masc|Person[app]=3` (ti_ref_110)
* ሰትዩሉ / *sətyulu* "he drank from it" (instrument): `Gender=Masc|Gender[app]=Masc` (ti_ref_186)

### <a name="Fem">`Fem`</a>: feminine applied argument

#### Examples

* ገዚአላ / *gəziʔɛla* "I bought (it) for her" (beneficiary)
