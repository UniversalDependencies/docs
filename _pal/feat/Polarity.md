This feature takes the following values: Neg.

### <a name='Neg'>`Neg`: Negated
Only one word form is inherently marked as negative: _nēst_ "is not" (an amalgamation of Old Persian _nai asti_).
Polarity is not indicated for nominals (such as privative prefix _a(n)-_ or derivations with _abē_ "without, -less").
