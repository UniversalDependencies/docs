The deprel `conj:zoom` is used for dependency relation in which the second element is describing the referent more in details.

PNT [PASSÉ-PROCHE] [DIMANCHE]-[AVANT] [FAMILLE] [RÉUNIR] [MANGER] [BAVARDER] [ÉCHANGER] \n Last sunday, all the family gathers to have a good meal, chat and exchange.
conj:zoom ([PASSÉ-PROCHE],[DIMANCHE]-[AVANT])
