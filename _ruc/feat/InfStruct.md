Ruuli uses overt marking for certain types of focus and topic.

### <a name="Foc">`Foc`</a>: focus

Focus can be marked with one of two clitics, _na_ or _mbe_, or a construction with the copula _ni_.
The clitic _na_ marks additive focus (translated as "also" or "even"). 
_mbe_ is commonly used for predicate focus.
The copula _ni_ is used for narrow focus.

#### Examples

* _wee<b>na</b>_ "**even** you (**also** you)"
* _balikwewunyanga<b>mbe</b>_ "they will **just** be surprised"
* _<b>ni</b>kyo nafumbiriirwe_ "**that is why** I married"

### <a name="Top">`Top`</a>: topic

The clitic _te_ is commonly used for contrastive topic.

#### Examples

* _nje<b>te</b>_ "as for me"
