In Cuicatec, this feature applies to [adverbs](cux-pos/ADV).

### <a name="Rel">`Rel`</a>: relative adverb

The relative adverb _nochi_ "donde" introduces a clause and refers to a place in it. The clause
can modify a noun ("la caja donde…") or the main clause ("…donde se derrumba"). _nochi_ is
attached to the verb of the clause it introduces as [advmod](cux-dep/advmod).

#### Examples

* [cux] _¡Ted kaja <b>nochi</b> jinabad kerillo ú!_ "¡Dame la caja donde guardas los cerillos!"
* [cux] _Kàba jí <b>nochi</b> jaá._ "Se pone feo donde se derrumba."
* [cux] _<b>Nochi</b> inbakuyan jiíy jadi._ "Donde se casan toman tepache."
