A negative adverbial modifier is a particle that modifies the root of a sentence adverbially. In Old Egyptian, nꞽ is a negative adverbial modifier.

3	⸗k	k	PRON	_	Gender=Masc|Number=Sing|Person=2|PronType=Prs	2	nsubj	_	Hiero=𓎡|Pron=SFP
