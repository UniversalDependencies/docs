The `nmod:appos` relation is used for appositions which modify nominal elements. 

#### Examples

* _á lə̂p gyáː Sommíɗa Ɓóngaː_ "at Sommida's place, Bonga"

nmod:appos (lə̂p, Ɓóngaː)
