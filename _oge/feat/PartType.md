The `PartType` feature typically applies to [particles](_oge/pos/PART), [pronouns](_oge/pos/PRON), [adpositions](_oge/pos/ADP), [nouns](_oge/pos/NOUN), [proper nouns](_oge/pos/PROPN) [adverbs](_oge/pos/ADV), [adjectives](_oge/pos/ADJ), [numerals](_oge/pos/NUM).

### <a name="Int">`Int`</a>: question particle

#### Examples

* _ნუუკუე_ 'not merely', _არამე_ 'rather' etc.

### <a name="Inf">`Inf`</a>: indefinite particle used mainly with pronouns

#### Examples

* _რომელი<b>მე</b>_ 'any', _ვინ<b>მე</b>_ 'anyone' etc.

### <a name="Neg">`Neg`</a>: negative particle

#### Examples

* _არ_ 'not', _ვერ_ 'not' etc.

### <a name="Ta">`Ta`</a>: affirmative particle

#### Examples

* _ჰე, ჰჱ_ 'yes' etc.

### <a name="Emp">`Emp`</a>: emphasis particle

#### Examples

* _ცა-ესე_ 'namely this', _ცა-ეგე_ 'namely that' etc.
